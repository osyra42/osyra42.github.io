# Baked Chicken Legs

**Ready to serve in:** 45 min

**Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 40 min | **Yields:** 3–5 servings (8–10 drumsticks)

## Ingredients

### Spice Rub

- [ ] **1 tbsp** Diamond kosher salt (or **2 tsp** table salt or other kosher salt)
- [ ] **2 tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **2 tsp** paprika or smoked paprika
- [ ] **1 tsp** brown sugar
- [ ] **1 tsp** dried oregano
- [ ] **1 tsp** ground cumin
- [ ] **½ tsp** crushed red pepper
- [ ] **½ tsp** freshly ground black pepper

### Chicken

- [ ] **8–10** chicken drumsticks (3½–4 lbs total)
- [ ] **1 tbsp** olive oil or neutral oil (such as avocado or grapeseed)
- [ ] **1 tbsp** chopped cilantro or parsley

## Instructions

### 1. Make the spice rub
Put all the spice rub ingredients in a small bowl. Mix well with a fork or small whisk, breaking up any clumps of sugar.

### 2. Prep the oven and pan
Arrange an oven rack to the **center position**. Preheat the oven to **425°F (220°C)**. Line a baking sheet with parchment or foil, or use a nonstick sheet pan. For even crispier skin, set a wire rack over the sheet pan so air can circulate on all sides.

### 3. Dry and oil the chicken
Pat the chicken legs with a paper towel to remove excess moisture. Arrange them on the prepared baking sheet so they don't touch. Drizzle with the oil, turning to coat.

### 4. Season
Sprinkle half the spice rub on one side of the chicken legs, then turn them over and sprinkle with the remaining rub. Turn them a few times to coat evenly in the spices and oil.

### 5. Bake
Bake for **20 min**. Use tongs to flip the chicken legs over. Return to the oven and bake an additional **20–25 min**. The meat should feel tender when pierced with a fork or the tip of a knife, and the skin should be crisp and starting to pull away from the meat. If you're not sure, an instant-read thermometer should read between **165–175°F (75–80°C)**.

### 6. Serve
Scatter the cilantro over the chicken. Serve warm, with the pan juices and lots of napkins!

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with a simple salad or roasted vegetables for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with crispy smashed potatoes with garlic and rosemary and Parmesan roasted Brussels sprouts — both cook at high heat, so you can run them alongside the chicken with minimal juggling.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for **up to 4 days**.
- **Reheat:** Warm on a baking sheet at **350°F** until heated through, about **10 min**.

## Notes

- **Why it works:** The drumstick's central bone conducts heat from the inside out, so the meat cooks from two directions at once — that's what keeps it succulent at high heat in just 40 min.
- **The spice rub sweet spot:**
  - **Brown sugar** caramelizes at high heat to create the lacquered, slightly crunchy exterior.
  - **Cumin** adds an earthy base note.
  - **Crushed red pepper** at **½ tsp** adds heat that builds slowly without overpowering.
  - **Paprika** — a **50-50 mix of regular and smoked** is ideal if you have both. Smoked paprika alone can taste bitter if overdone.
- **Oil matters:** A light coating of oil between the rub and the skin binds the spices and promotes browning. Olive oil works, but neutral high-heat oils like avocado or grapeseed are also good.
- **Make the rub ahead:** The spice rub can be mixed and stored in a sealed container at room temperature for **up to 3 months**. Double or triple the batch to keep on hand.
- **Prep ahead:** Apply the oil and spice rub to the chicken and refrigerate overnight. Bring to room temperature before baking.
- **Chicken thighs:** Substitute bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs. Depending on their size, they may need an additional **5–10 min** baking time.
- **Air fryer option:** Place the seasoned chicken in the air fryer basket in batches (don't crowd). Cook **20–25 min**, turning halfway.
- **If the skin isn't crisping:** One reader's oven ran cool at 425°F and needed 450°F. Another trick — briefly broil the chicken legs at the end to crisp them up to your liking.
- **Season the outside only:** One reader seasoned under the skin and found the flavor didn't come through — the rub needs to be on the outside so it caramelizes into a crust.
