# Chicken Parmesan

**Ready to serve in:** 35 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 25 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **2 large** chicken breasts (about **1½ lbs**), sliced into 4 cutlets
- [ ] **½ cup** all-purpose flour (or GF flour)
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt
- [ ] **½ tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **2 large** eggs, beaten until frothy
- [ ] **½ cup** Italian bread crumbs
- [ ] **½ cup** Parmesan cheese, shredded or grated
- [ ] **2 tbsp** light olive oil, for sautéing
- [ ] **3 cups** marinara sauce, divided
- [ ] **4 oz (1 cup shredded)** mozzarella cheese
- [ ] **1 tbsp** fresh basil or parsley, chopped, for garnish *(optional)*

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the chicken
Trim chicken breasts and slice in half lengthwise so you end up with 4 equal-thickness cutlets. Pound larger pieces between plastic wrap with a meat mallet to an even **⅓"** thickness. Season with salt and pepper.

### 2. Set up a dredging station
Set out three shallow bowls:
- **Bowl 1:** Stir together the flour, **1 tsp** salt, and **½ tsp** black pepper.
- **Bowl 2:** Whisk the eggs until frothy.
- **Bowl 3:** Stir together the Italian bread crumbs and Parmesan cheese.

Dip both sides of each cutlet into the flour, tapping off the excess. Then dip in the beaten eggs, letting excess drip off. Finally, dip into the cheesy breadcrumbs, pressing the cutlets in to ensure a thick, even coating. Let the breading sit for **5 min** so it adheres better when frying.

### 3. Pan-fry
Heat a large nonstick pan over **medium heat** and add enough olive oil to lightly coat the bottom. Once hot, add the chicken in batches (don't crowd the pan) — it should sizzle. Sauté **3–4 min per side**, until golden brown. It doesn't have to be cooked through at this point; it'll finish in the oven.

### 4. Assemble
Pour half the marinara into a 9×13" casserole dish. Arrange the chicken over the sauce in a single layer. Top each piece down the center with the remaining marinara, then cover with shredded mozzarella.

### 5. Bake
Bake uncovered at **425°F** for **15 min**, or until the chicken reaches **165°F** on an instant-read thermometer, the cheese is melted, and the sauce is bubbling. Garnish with chopped parsley or basil if desired.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with roasted asparagus or roasted cauliflower for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve over buttered pasta (spaghetti works great) with a Caesar salad on the side. Also excellent as a hot sandwich on a toasted hamburger bun.

## Storage Tips

- **Prep ahead:** You can sauté the breaded chicken until fully cooked to **165°F**, arrange it in a casserole dish, and top with marinara and cheese. Cover and refrigerate for **up to 24 hrs**. Bake before serving, adding **5 min** to the bake time (or until the internal temp reaches **165°F**).
- **Leftovers:** Cool to room temperature, cover, and refrigerate for **up to 3 days**. Or wrap and freeze in a freezer-safe container for **up to 2 months**.
- **Reheat:** Thaw in the refrigerator overnight if frozen, then bake uncovered at **375°F** so the crust crisps up.

## Notes

- **Chicken prep:** Chicken cutlets or chicken tenders also work and skip the pounding step.
- **Breading swaps:** Panko, plain bread crumbs, or gluten-free bread crumbs all work. Add **½ tsp** dried oregano for extra flavor.
- **Egg-free option:** Replace the eggs with buttermilk for dredging.
- **Cheese:** Freshly grated Parmesan for the crust and shredded mozzarella for topping. Whole milk or low-moisture part-skim mozzarella both work. A slice of fresh mozzarella over the top is nice but will release more moisture.
- **Extra crispy version:** Reduce or omit the marinara at the bottom of the casserole dish. You can also bake the chicken on a wire rack set over a baking sheet for crispness all around. The author's personal preference is to keep the sauce — it's moisture insurance, prevents sticking, and makes it a one-pan dish ready to serve over spaghetti.
- **Baked (not fried) version:** Complete steps 1 and 2. Coat the breaded chicken with cooking spray on all sides and bake on a baking sheet at **400°F for 15 min**, or until the center reaches **165°F**. Remove from the oven, add **2–3 tbsp** marinara over each cutlet, top with cheese, and return to the oven for **3–5 min** until the cheese is melted.
- **Marinara:** Homemade marinara is best, but store-bought works in a pinch.
