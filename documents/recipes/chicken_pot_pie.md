# Chicken Pot Pie

**Ready to serve in:** 6 hrs 5 min

**Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 6 hrs | **Yields:** 8 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **3** boneless chicken breasts
- [ ] **1 can (10.5 oz)** cream of chicken soup
- [ ] **1 cup** milk
- [ ] **½** onion, chopped
- [ ] **4** potatoes, peeled and diced
- [ ] **16 oz bag** frozen mixed vegetables
- [ ] **½ cup** chopped celery
- [ ] **1 tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** poultry seasoning
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt
- [ ] **½ tsp** pepper
- [ ] **16 oz** Grands biscuits (8 count)

## Instructions

### 1. Combine in the crockpot
Add the chicken breasts, cream of chicken soup, milk, onion, potatoes, frozen mixed vegetables, celery, garlic powder, poultry seasoning, salt, and pepper to the crockpot. Stir to combine.

### 2. Cook
Cover and cook on **LOW for 6–8 hrs** or **HIGH for 3–4 hrs**, until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are fork-tender.

### 3. Shred the chicken
Remove the chicken from the crockpot and shred it with two forks.

### 4. Return and stir
Return the shredded chicken to the crockpot and stir until combined.

### 5. Bake the biscuits
Bake the biscuits according to the package directions.

### 6. Serve
Spoon the chicken pot pie mixture into bowls and top each serving with a warm biscuit. Serve and enjoy!

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve in a bowl without the biscuit, or with a smaller biscuit on top, for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Top each bowl with a warm biscuit. Refrigerated biscuits (like Grands) are the easiest option, but homemade drop biscuits or Bisquick biscuits work great too.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for **up to 5–7 days**.
- **Freezer meal prep:** Toss all ingredients **except the potatoes** into a freezer bag and freeze. When ready to cook, empty the bag into the crockpot, add the potatoes, and cook as normal. (Potatoes don't freeze well uncooked.)
- **If you want to freeze the potatoes too:** Substitute diced hash browns for the potatoes — they'll freeze and cook just as well.

## Notes

- **Frozen chicken works:** No need to thaw — just add **30–60 min** to the cook time on LOW to make sure it's cooked through.
- **Cubed chicken works too:** If you cube the chicken instead of leaving the breasts whole, start checking earlier since smaller pieces cook faster. Make sure the chicken reaches **165°F** and the potatoes are tender.
- **Thicken it up:** If the filling is thinner than you'd like, make a cornstarch slurry — whisk **¼ cup** cornstarch with **1 cup** cold water or chicken broth until dissolved, stir it into the crockpot, and cook on **HIGH for 30 min** until thickened. You can also do this with flour. (For a double batch, start with **2 tbsp** cornstarch + **2 tbsp** cold water.)
- **Why it might be watery:** It may just need more time — the filling sets up as it cooks. If the top is getting too dark or burning, place a piece of foil over it.
- **Fresh vegetables work:** Fresh carrots, celery, or other veggies can be used. Add fresh chopped carrots at the beginning with the other ingredients so they have time to get tender.
- **Creamy version:** Mix in heavy cream instead of the milk, but don't add it until the **last hour** of cook time.
- **Homemade cream of chicken:** You can make your own cream of chicken soup to replace the canned version and make this recipe healthier.
- **Chicken thighs:** Work great in this recipe — cube them and trim the fat.
- **Veggie swaps:** Use whatever vegetables you love. Green beans work well. If you don't like carrots, celery, corn, or peas, leave them out and add your favorites.
- **Biscuit options:** Any biscuit works — refrigerated, homemade drop biscuits, or Bisquick biscuits.
- **Flavor boost:** One reader found the base recipe a bit bland and recommends marinating the chicken in chicken spice beforehand, doubling the seasonings (except salt and pepper), and adding gluten-free bouillon for extra flavor.
