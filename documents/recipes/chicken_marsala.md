# Chicken Marsala

**Ready to serve in:** 45 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 30 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1½ lbs** boneless skinless chicken breasts, pounded ¼" thick (or chicken tenderloins)
- [ ] **3 tbsp** all-purpose flour
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] **1 tbsp** olive oil
- [ ] **3 tbsp** unsalted butter, divided
- [ ] **1 (8-oz) package** pre-sliced bella or button mushrooms
- [ ] **3 tbsp** finely chopped shallots (from 1 medium shallot)
- [ ] **2 cloves** garlic, minced
- [ ] **⅔ cup** chicken broth
- [ ] **⅔ cup** dry Marsala wine
- [ ] **⅔ cup** heavy cream
- [ ] **2 tsp** chopped fresh thyme
- [ ] **2 tbsp** chopped fresh Italian parsley, for serving *(optional)*

## Instructions

### 1. Coat the chicken
Place the flour, **¾ tsp** salt, and **¼ tsp** pepper in a ziplock bag. Add the chicken to the bag; seal tightly and shake to coat evenly. Set aside.

### 2. Sear the chicken
Heat the oil and **2 tbsp** of the butter in a large skillet over **medium-high heat**. (Use a stainless steel pan for the best browning — nonstick works too, but you won't get that nice golden color.) Place the flour-dusted chicken in the pan, shaking off any excess first, and cook, turning once, until golden and just barely cooked through, about **5–6 min total**. Transfer to a plate and set aside.

### 3. Sauté the mushrooms
Melt the remaining **1 tbsp** butter in the pan. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring frequently, until they begin to brown, **3–4 min**.

### 4. Add the aromatics
Add the shallots, garlic, and **¼ tsp** salt; cook for **1–2 min** more.

### 5. Build the sauce
Add the broth, Marsala, heavy cream, thyme, **¼ tsp** salt, and **⅛ tsp** pepper; use a wooden spoon to scrape any brown bits from the pan into the liquid. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to **medium** and gently boil, uncovered, until the sauce is reduced by about half, slightly thickened, and darkened in color, **10–15 min**. (You're going for a thin cream sauce — it won't start to thicken until the very end of the cooking time.)

### 6. Finish the chicken
Add the chicken back to the pan, along with any juices that accumulated on the plate. Reduce the heat to **low** and simmer until the chicken is warmed through and the sauce thickens a bit more, **2–3 min**.

### 7. Serve
Sprinkle with parsley, if using, and serve.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve over polenta or with a side of French green beans with shallots for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve over pasta, rice, or Parmesan smashed potatoes to soak up the sauce.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for **up to 3 days**.
- Reheat gently in a skillet over low heat, adding a splash of broth or cream if the sauce tightens up too much.
- Freezing is **not recommended** — cream sauces can separate when thawed.

## Notes

- **Chicken prep:** If your breasts are large (about ¾ lb each), cut them horizontally first to form four flat fillets, then pound to an even **¼"** thickness. Pounding large breasts without halving them first leaves them huge and oddly shaped.
- **Marsala** is a brandy-fortified wine from Sicily. It's worth keeping in the pantry — it stores in a cool, dry spot for months.
- **Shortcut:** Pre-sliced mushrooms save time; chicken tenderloins skip the pounding step entirely since they're naturally tender.
- **Don't skip the cream** — the sauce relies on it to come together.
