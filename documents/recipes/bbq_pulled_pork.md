# BBQ Pulled Pork

**Ready to serve in:** 10 hrs 15 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 10 hrs | **Yields:** 12–20 servings

## Ingredients

### For the Pork

- [ ] **3.5–4.5 kg / 5–7 lb** pork butt or pork shoulder, bone-in, fat cap on
- [ ] **1 cup** beer, apple cider, or apple juice

### For the Rub

- [ ] **3 tbsp** brown sugar
- [ ] **1 tbsp** paprika powder
- [ ] **1 tbsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **2 tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **2 tsp** mustard powder
- [ ] **1 tsp** cumin powder
- [ ] **1 tbsp** salt
- [ ] **1 tsp** black pepper

### For the BBQ Sauce

- [ ] **1 cup** apple cider vinegar
- [ ] **3 cups** tomato ketchup
- [ ] **1 cup** water (or use remaining beer)
- [ ] **3 tbsp** molasses, original (not blackstrap)
- [ ] **⅔ cup** brown sugar
- [ ] **4 tsp** mustard powder
- [ ] **3 tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **4 tsp** Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] **2 tsp** salt
- [ ] **2 tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **2 tsp** Tabasco or cayenne pepper *(optional, for spiciness)*

## Instructions

### 1. Rub the pork
Mix the rub ingredients together, then rub all over the pork.

### 2. Slow cook
Place in a slow cooker and pour over the beer. Slow cook on **LOW for 10 hrs**. The pork should be tender enough to easily shred.

### 3. Brown in the oven *(optional, recommended)*
Preheat oven to **180°C / 350°F**. Transfer the pork from the slow cooker into a roasting pan (reserve the liquid). Scoop the fat off the surface of the juices in the slow cooker and drizzle over the pork (about **⅓ cup**), then roast for **20 min**. Remove from the oven, cut the thick layer of fat off the top and discard. Spoon over **¼ cup** fat from the slow cooker juices, then roast **15 min** more.

### 4. Make the BBQ sauce
Place all sauce ingredients in a saucepan and simmer over **medium heat**, stirring occasionally, for **45 min** or until thickened. Add **¾–1 cup** of the juices from the slow cooker until the sauce reaches a syrup consistency. Adjust to taste — sweetness with brown sugar or honey, salt, and sour with vinegar.

### 5. Shred and combine
Shred the pork using 2 forks. Pour over the BBQ sauce and toss gently — you want tender, juicy pork, not mush.

### 6. Serve
Serve stuffed in sliders with coleslaw, or build a Southern dinner plate with coleslaw, mac and cheese, cornbread, steamed corn, and green beans tossed in butter.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve over a bed of greens or with extra coleslaw for a lower-carb plate. Skip the rolls and cornbread.
- **For the family:** Pile into soft slider rolls with coleslaw (makes around **20 sliders**), or plate up a full Southern BBQ spread with mac and cheese, cornbread, and steamed corn.

## Storage Tips

- Freezes brilliantly, even after tossing with sauce.
- Keeps in the fridge for **up to 4 days**.
- Best reheated in the microwave to keep things juicy and moist.

## Nutrition

Per serving, pork and sauce only (from the source):
- **416** calories
- **11.3g** fat (3.8g saturated)
- **37.7g** carbs (0.6g fiber, 31.9g sugar)
- **36.9g** protein
- **113mg** cholesterol | **1,666mg** sodium

*Note: the source cautions these calories are overstated — a lot of fat is discarded with the slow cooker juices.*

## Notes

- **Best cut:** Pork butt (aka Boston Butt) or pork shoulder. Both are well marbled and need a long cook to become shreddable. Get bone-in if you can. Leave the thick fat layer on — it self-bastes the meat as it cooks. If there's skin, remove it so the spice can infuse better (not the end of the world if you don't).
- **Size matters:** Don't go much below **2 kg / 4 lb** or the pork cooks too fast and lacks time to infuse flavor. Use the recipe scaler if scaling down. A 3.5 kg / 7 lb bone-in shoulder feeds **12** as a plate with sides, or **20** as sliders.
- **Liquid:** Any beer except dark beers like stout or Guinness. Hard or non-alcoholic cider works great, as does apple juice. Water is the emergency fallback.
- **Vinegar:** White wine or champagne vinegar also works. Plain white vinegar can be used but reduce by **1 tbsp** since it's sharper.
- **Molasses** adds richness and deepens the sauce color. Don't use blackstrap.
- **Don't over-shred:** Cook for the right length of time (10 hrs on low is enough) and toss gently with sauce. You want tender meat with texture, not baby food.
- **Other cook methods:**
  - **Pressure cooker / Instant Pot:** With a smaller pork (2–2.5 kg / 4–5 lb), cook **1 hr 20 min** on high.
  - **Oven:** **7–8 hrs at 130°C / 270°F**, covered with double foil in a roasting pan with 2 cups water (plus the beer). Slow cooker yields juicier pork, but the oven gives an amazing crust.
