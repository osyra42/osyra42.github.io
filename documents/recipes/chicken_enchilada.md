# Chicken Enchiladas

**Ready to serve in:** 30 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1½ cups** cooked shredded chicken
- [ ] **2 cups** easy enchilada sauce, divided
- [ ] **8** corn or flour tortillas
- [ ] **2½ cups** shredded Mexican-blend cheese, divided
- [ ] Salt and black pepper, to taste

### Optional Toppings

- [ ] Diced onions
- [ ] Chopped cilantro
- [ ] Sour cream
- [ ] Shredded lettuce
- [ ] Cotija cheese

## Instructions

### 1. Prep and season the chicken
Preheat oven to **350°F**. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken, **¼ cup** enchilada sauce, and a generous pinch of salt and pepper. Mix together and taste. Season with more salt and pepper as needed.

### 2. Warm the tortillas
**If using corn tortillas:** Wrap them in a damp paper towel and heat in the microwave for **1 min**, flipping them halfway through, until warm and pliable.
**If using flour tortillas:** Microwave them on a plate for **1 min**, flipping halfway through, until warm and pliable.

### 3. Assemble
Fill each tortilla evenly with the shredded chicken mixture and **1 cup** of the shredded cheese. Roll the tortillas tightly to close and place them seam-side down in a large baking dish.

### 4. Top and bake
Pour the remaining **1¾ cups** enchilada sauce over the tortillas, top with the remaining **1½ cups** shredded cheese, and bake for **20 min**, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.

### 5. Serve
Serve immediately and garnish with desired toppings.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with a side of guacamole and a simple salad for a lower-carb plate. Skip the rice.
- **For the family:** Serve with Mexican rice or arroz verde, a scoop of creamy refried beans, and a side of guacamole. Top with diced tomatoes, avocado slices, diced onions, and shredded lettuce for a well-rounded meal.

## Storage Tips

- **Refrigerate:** Store in an airtight container for **up to 4 days**.
- **Freeze:** Let cool completely in the fridge, then transfer to a freezer-safe container or freezer bag and freeze. You can also wrap the enchiladas completely in aluminum foil before freezing.

## Notes

- **Chicken:** Use any leftover shredded chicken you have in the fridge, a store-bought rotisserie chicken, or make your own shredded chicken at home.
- **Enchilada sauce:** Homemade is best. Two options on the site — an easy enchilada sauce (chili powder, tomato paste, broth) or an authentic enchilada sauce (whole dried chiles and Mexican chocolate). Store-bought works in a pinch.
- **Tortillas:** Corn tortillas are traditional. Flour tortillas work too, but they're often larger, so you'll need an additional **½ cup** of cooked shredded chicken for the filling.
- **Cheese:** Pre-shredded "Mexican-style" blend is easy and works well. Cheddar, mozzarella, or pepperjack also work. For a more authentic option, try Oaxaca (quesillo), Chihuahua, or Asadero.
- **Traditional method (more authentic):** Shallow fry the corn tortillas in a little oil for **5–10 seconds per side** to make them pliable and prevent sogginess. Drain off excess oil, then dip each fried tortilla into warm enchilada sauce. Place the smothered tortilla on a plate, add the filling, and roll it up. Top with any remaining enchilada sauce and a sprinkle of queso fresco or cotija cheese.
- **Sauce variations:** Green enchilada sauce, salsa verde, or a cheesy white sauce all work.
- **Meat variations:** Shredded carnitas, barbacoa, or birria all work great. Picadillo or taco meat would too.
- **Add beans or veggies:** Black beans, pinto beans, or sautéed zucchini and bell peppers are great additions to the shredded chicken.
- **Reader additions that work:** Corn, black beans, salsa, canned chicken (for a shortcut), and cheddar cheese.
