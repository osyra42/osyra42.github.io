# Egg Salad

**Ready to serve in:** 35 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 15 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **8 large** eggs
- [ ] **½ cup** mayonnaise
- [ ] **¼ cup** chopped green onion
- [ ] **1 tsp** prepared yellow mustard
- [ ] **¼ tsp** paprika
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

### 1. Hard-boil the eggs
Place eggs in a saucepan and cover with cold water. Bring water to a boil and immediately remove from heat. Cover and let eggs stand in hot water for **10–12 min**.

### 2. Cool and chop
Remove eggs from hot water; cool, peel, and chop.

### 3. Mix the salad
Place chopped eggs in a bowl; stir in mayonnaise, green onion, and mustard. Season with paprika, salt, and pepper.

### 4. Serve
Stir and serve on your favorite bread, crackers, or salad greens.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Spoon over a bed of salad greens, or serve in a lettuce wrap for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Pile onto rye bread or whole grain bread with lettuce for classic egg salad sandwiches. Pairs well with potato chips, dill pickles, or a spinach salad.

## Storage Tips

- Store leftovers in a shallow, airtight container in the refrigerator for **up to 4 days**.
- **Freezing is not recommended** — it changes the texture and flavor. If you don't mind that, it can be frozen for up to **1 month** and thawed overnight in the refrigerator.

## Notes

- **Green onion amount:** The recipe author notes that **¼ cup** is what the card says, but she personally uses closer to **2 tbsp** — adjust to your preference.
- **This is a great base recipe.** It's designed to be built on. Common additions from reviewers: finely diced celery, dill weed, pickle relish or chopped pickles, Dijon mustard in place of yellow, chives or shallots instead of green onion, diced red onion, or a dash of hot sauce.
- **Mix the wet ingredients first:** Whisk the mayo, mustard, onion, salt, pepper, and paprika together before adding to the eggs — it blends the flavors and keeps the eggs from getting broken up by over-stirring.
- **Easier peeling:** Eggs that are a week or more old peel more easily than very fresh ones. An egg slicer makes quick work of chopping.
- **Alternative cooking methods:** Several reviewers swear by the air fryer (**250°F for 20 min** for extra-large eggs, then a **10-min** ice water bath) or boiling for **15 min** then into ice water for **15 min**.
- **Less runny:** Some reviewers swap in **3–4 oz** cream cheese plus **2 tbsp** mayo instead of all mayo for a thicker texture.
