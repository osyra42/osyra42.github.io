# Tuna Salad

**Ready to serve in:** 15 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 0 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1 (5-oz) can** tuna, drained
- [ ] **½ cup** mayonnaise
- [ ] **¼ cup** chopped celery
- [ ] **¼ cup** chopped onion
- [ ] **1 tbsp** chopped fresh parsley
- [ ] **½ tsp** lemon juice
- [ ] **¼ tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **⅛ tsp** salt
- [ ] **⅛ tsp** ground black pepper
- [ ] **1 pinch** paprika, or to taste

## Instructions

### 1. Combine
Combine the tuna, mayonnaise, celery, onion, parsley, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Mix well.

### 2. Season and chill
Season with paprika, then refrigerate until chilled.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve on top of a green salad or in a lettuce wrap for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve between two pieces of bread with fresh lettuce, or with crackers on the side.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate in an airtight container for **up to 3 days**. Stir before serving.

## Nutrition

Per serving (from the source):
- **240** calories
- **22g** fat
- **2g** carbs
- **9g** protein

## Notes

- **Mayo ratio:** Multiple reviewers found **½ cup** mayo too much for one 5-oz can of tuna. If you prefer a firmer, less mayo-forward salad, use **3–4 tbsp** mayo instead, or add a second can of tuna.
- **Mild onion:** Red onion is milder than white. If using white onion, some reviewers preferred only **2 tbsp**. Dice onion and celery small so they spread evenly through the mix.
- **Drain the tuna well.** Extra water makes the salad mushy — use a strainer if needed.
- **Tuna in oil vs. water:** Some readers prefer tuna packed in oil for richer flavor.
- **Add-ins that work well:**
  - **3 chopped hard-boiled eggs** (a popular addition)
  - **½–1 tbsp** red wine vinegar for extra brightness
  - **2 tbsp** sweet relish or pickle relish
  - **A hint of mustard** (yellow or Dijon)
  - **Diced pickled jalapeños** with a splash of the vinegar juice for heat
  - **Grapes and nuts** for a sweeter, crunchier version
- **Mayo swaps:** Greek yogurt can replace half the mayo; ranch dressing works too (reduce or omit the salt if using ranch).
- **Paprika on top** adds both flavor and color — don't skip it.
- **Tastes even better the next day**, per several reviewers.
