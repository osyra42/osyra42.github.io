# Chocolate Banana Bread

**Ready to serve in:** 1 hr

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 50 min | **Yields:** 12 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1 cup** all-purpose flour
- [ ] **½ cup** Dutch process cocoa
- [ ] **1 tsp** baking soda
- [ ] **½ tsp** sea salt
- [ ] **3 large** brown bananas (**1½ cups** mashed)
- [ ] **¼ cup** unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
- [ ] **¼ cup** canola, vegetable, or melted coconut oil
- [ ] **¾ cup** packed light brown sugar
- [ ] **1 large** egg, at room temperature
- [ ] **1 tsp** pure vanilla extract
- [ ] **1 cup** semisweet chocolate chips, divided

## Instructions

### 1. Prep
Heat your oven to **350°F**. Grease a 9×5" loaf pan with nonstick cooking spray and set aside.

### 2. Mix the dry ingredients
In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and sea salt. Set aside.

### 3. Mix the wet ingredients
In a large bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork. Add the melted butter and oil and stir until combined. Stir in the brown sugar, egg, and vanilla extract. Stir until smooth.

### 4. Combine
Stir the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients — don't overmix. Stir in **¾ cup** of the chocolate chips.

### 5. Bake
Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Sprinkle the remaining **¼ cup** chocolate chips over the top. Bake for **50–65 min**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out mostly clean. You might have some melted chocolate chips on the toothpick — that's fine, you just don't want a lot of gooey batter. Check at **50 min** just to be safe; oven times vary.

### 6. Cool and serve
Remove the pan from the oven and set on a wire cooling rack. Let the bread cool in the pan for **15 min**. Run a knife around the edges and carefully remove from the pan. Let it cool on the rack until slightly warm. Cut into slices and serve.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** A slice on its own is perfect — it's rich enough that you don't need anything else.
- **For the family:** Serve warm with a pat of butter, or top a slice with vanilla ice cream for dessert. Also great alongside coffee or a glass of cold milk.

## Storage Tips

- Store on the counter, wrapped in plastic wrap, for **up to 4 days**.
- **Freeze:** Cool completely, wrap in plastic wrap and aluminum foil, and freeze for **up to 1 month**. Defrost before slicing.

## Notes

- **Bananas are the key:** Overripe, brown bananas make this bread. **1½ cups** mashed is the magic number — it keeps the bread super moist while still letting you taste the banana alongside the chocolate.
- **Dutch process cocoa matters:** It gives a deeper, more intense chocolate flavor than regular cocoa. If you only have unsweetened cocoa, it'll still be good — just less intense.
- **Butter + oil both:** Don't swap one for the other. The butter gives flavor, the oil keeps it moist, and the combo produces a tender crumb.
- **Brown sugar only:** The molasses content in brown sugar keeps baked goods extra moist.
- **Sea salt is the kicker:** Regular salt works, but sea salt gives little bursts of salty contrast against the chocolate.
- **Don't overbake:** A toothpick should come out clean or mostly clean. If you hit a chocolate chip, the melted chocolate on the toothpick isn't a sign of raw batter. Test in a few spots.
- **Muffin version:** Works great as muffins — reduce the baking time to **18–24 min**.
- **Substitutions that work:**
  - **Coconut oil** in place of vegetable oil adds a subtle flavor (many readers prefer it).
  - **Gluten-free flour** and **coconut sugar** in place of the brown sugar both work (per readers).
  - **Butterscotch chips, caramel chips, or a shot of Kahlua/bourbon** are excellent add-ins.
- **One reader's note:** Reducing the sea salt by half if you're sensitive to salt — one reviewer found the full amount overpowering.
- **Pan note:** If you don't have a 9×5" loaf pan, a brownie/cake pan works and bakes in about **30 min** at 350°F.
