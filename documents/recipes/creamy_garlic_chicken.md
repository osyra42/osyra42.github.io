# Creamy Garlic Chicken

**Ready to serve in:** 30 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Yields:** ~4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **2** boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 6–8 oz each)
- [ ] Salt, pepper, and garlic powder (for seasoning)
- [ ] **2 tbsp** olive oil or butter (for searing)
- [ ] **4 cloves** garlic, minced (or **1 tsp** garlic powder)
- [ ] **1 cup** heavy cream (full-fat – fewer carbs than milk)
- [ ] **½ cup** low-sodium chicken broth
- [ ] **½ cup** grated Parmesan cheese
- [ ] **2 big handfuls** fresh spinach (or **½ cup** frozen, thawed and drained)
- [ ] *Optional:* **1 tsp** Italian seasoning or fresh parsley for garnish

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the chicken
Slice each breast horizontally (butterfly) to make **4 thin cutlets**, or pound them to even thickness (about **½ inch**). Season both sides generously with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.

### 2. Sear
Heat olive oil or butter in a large skillet over **medium-high heat**. Add chicken and cook **5–6 minutes per side**, until golden brown and cooked through (internal temp **165°F**). Remove chicken to a plate and cover loosely with foil.

### 3. Make the sauce
Turn heat down to **medium**. Add minced garlic to the pan and sauté for **30–60 seconds** until fragrant (don't burn it!). Pour in chicken broth and scrape up any browned bits from the pan. Let it simmer for **2 minutes** to reduce slightly.

### 4. Add cream and cheese
Pour in the heavy cream and stir. Add the Parmesan cheese and whisk until smooth. Let it simmer for **3–4 minutes** until it thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.

### 5. Add spinach
Stir in the fresh spinach and cook for **1–2 minutes** until wilted. (If using frozen, stir it in and heat through.)

### 6. Combine
Return the chicken to the skillet, spooning some sauce over the top. Let it warm through for **1–2 minutes**.

### 7. Serve
Garnish with parsley or a sprinkle of extra Parmesan.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve over steamed green beans, broccoli, or cauliflower rice.
- **For the family:** Serve over fettuccine, pasta, or mashed potatoes (on the side).

## Nutrition

**Your carbs:** ~**5–7g** per serving (from the spinach and cream – Parmesan and chicken are zero).
