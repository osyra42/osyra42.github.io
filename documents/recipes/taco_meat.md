# Taco Meat

**Ready to serve in:** 15 min

**Prep:** 0 min | **Cook:** 15 min | **Yields:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1 lb** lean ground beef (or ground chicken, turkey, or pork)
- [ ] **½ cup** tomato sauce
- [ ] **¼ cup** water
- [ ] **½ tbsp** chili powder
- [ ] **1 tsp** cumin
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt
- [ ] **½ tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** paprika
- [ ] **⅛ tsp** oregano
- [ ] **¼ tsp** pepper
- [ ] **⅛ tsp** crushed red pepper flakes

## Instructions

### 1. Brown the meat
Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over **medium-high heat**, crumbling it into small chunks as it cooks. Once completely browned, drain off any excess fat and blot with a paper towel to remove residual grease.

### 2. Add the sauce and seasonings
Return the meat to the heat and add the tomato sauce, water, chili powder, cumin, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, oregano, pepper, and crushed red pepper flakes.

### 3. Simmer
Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to **medium** and simmer for **5–7 min**, until the liquid from the tomatoes has been absorbed.

### 4. Serve
Serve as desired.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve over a taco salad with lettuce, diced tomatoes, olives, onions, and cheese, or pile into baked sweet potatoes for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve in soft or hard taco shells with cheese, lettuce, tomatoes, and sour cream. Also great for nachos, taco pizza, taco pasta, or wonton taco cups.

## Storage Tips

- Store cooled taco meat in a sealed container or storage bag for **3 days** in the refrigerator.
- Freezes well for **up to 3 months**. Portion into 1 lb or ½ lb servings in freezer-safe bags, squeeze out the air, label, and freeze. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat in a microwave-safe dish.
- **Make-ahead tip:** Double or triple the recipe and freeze portions for busy nights.

## Notes

- **Any ground meat works** — beef, chicken, turkey, pork, or even venison.
- **Shortcut:** In place of the individual spices, use **2 tbsp** homemade taco seasoning.
- **Lower sodium:** Omit the salt.
- **Slow cooker version:** Brown the meat, then place in a slow cooker with the tomato sauce and taco seasoning. Add **½ cup** water and cook on **LOW for 4 hrs**.
- **Restaurant-style texture:** After browning, pulse the meat a few times in a food processor until small crumbles form. Be careful not to over-process — you just want to break it down. Return to the skillet and continue.
- **Frozen meat:** Don't cook frozen ground meat in a skillet or defrost it in the microwave. Use an Instant Pot instead — cook the frozen meat, release pressure, drain the fat, then switch to sauté and add the seasonings, tomato sauce, and water. Sauté for **5 min** or until warmed through.
