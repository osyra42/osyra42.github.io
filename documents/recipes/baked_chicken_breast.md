# Oven Baked Chicken Breast

**Ready to serve in:** 30 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Yields:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **2 lbs** chicken breast, boneless and skinless
- [ ] **1 tbsp** olive oil
- [ ] **1 tbsp** light brown sugar
- [ ] **2 tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **2 tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **1 tsp** ground paprika
- [ ] **1 tsp** Italian seasoning
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt
- [ ] **¼ tsp** ground black pepper
- [ ] **1 tbsp** butter, diced

## Instructions

### 1. Prep
Heat oven to **400°F**. Lightly grease a 9×13" casserole dish.

### 2. Flatten
Place chicken between two pieces of plastic wrap. Using a rolling pin or the flat side of a mallet, pound chicken to about **0.5–0.75"** thickness. Rub olive oil all over the flattened chicken breasts.

### 3. Season
Combine all remaining ingredients **except the butter** in a small bowl. Sprinkle all over the chicken, then lightly rub it in.

### 4. Bake
Place the seasoned chicken in the prepared casserole dish. Top each breast with a slice of butter. Bake at **400°F** until the internal temperature reaches **165°F**, about **18–20 min**. Finish under the broiler for **1–3 min** until nicely browned. Serve immediately.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Slice and serve over a salad, or pair with sautéed zucchini or roasted asparagus for a lighter plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with mashed potatoes, Instant Pot mushroom risotto, bacon pea pasta, roasted broccoli, or roasted asparagus. The pan sauce that forms while baking is great tossed with rice or pasta.

## Storage Tips

- Store baked chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for **up to 3 days**.
- Great for meal prep — dice, shred, or slice the leftovers for casseroles, soups, salads, quesadillas, or enchiladas.
- Reheat gently to avoid drying it out.

## Notes

- **Don't skip the flattening.** It seems like an unnecessary extra step, but it makes a big difference to the end result — even thickness means even cooking.
- **Season all over,** front and back. That's how you get the most flavor into every bite.
- **Do not overbake.** A meat thermometer is the most accurate way to check for **165°F**. The US food safety guideline for all poultry is a minimum internal temperature of **165°F (74°C)**.
- **Ingredient swaps:**
  - **Butter:** Can be replaced with olive oil or skipped, though the chicken will be slightly less juicy and the seasoning won't be quite the same.
  - **Brown sugar:** Regular sugar works. Light brown is preferred over dark. Skipping it is fine, but it makes a noticeable difference to the taste.
  - **Seasonings:** Feel free to skip anything you don't like or experiment with your own blend.
- **Other cuts:** If you use a different cut than boneless skinless breast, the baking time will be different from this recipe.
- **Doubling the seasoning:** One reader doubles the spice mix so they have plenty on hand — works great.
