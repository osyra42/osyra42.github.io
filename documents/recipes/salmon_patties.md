# Salmon "Crab-Style" Patties

**Ready to serve in:** 20 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 10 min | **Yields:** 4 patties

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1 can** (14.75 oz) salmon, drained and flaked (keep the soft bones – mash 'em in for extra calcium!)
- [ ] **1 large** egg, beaten
- [ ] **¼ cup** almond flour **OR** crushed pork rinds (instead of breadcrumbs)
- [ ] **½ tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** paprika or Old Bay seasoning *(optional)*
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste
- [ ] **1–2 tbsp** olive oil or butter (for frying)

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the salmon
Drain the salmon thoroughly. Put it in a bowl and flake it with a fork. (Mash any soft bones right in – you won't notice them.)

### 2. Mix
Add the beaten egg, almond flour/pork rinds, and all seasonings. Mix until well combined.

### 3. Form patties
Form into **4 small patties** (about **½ inch** thick).

### 4. Fry
Heat olive oil or butter in a skillet over **medium heat**.

### 5. Cook
Cook patties for **3–4 minutes per side**, until golden brown and crispy.

### 6. Serve
Serve hot!

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Enjoy with a side salad, roasted vegetables, or over cauliflower rice for a low-carb meal.
- **For the family:** Serve alongside tartar sauce, lemon wedges, or fresh dill – pairs well with rice or a crusty roll.

## Nutrition

- **Low-carb** – no breadcrumbs, grains, or starches.
- **High-protein** – packed with salmon and egg.
- **Calcium boost** – from the mashed soft bones!
