# Slow Cook Chicken Breast

**Ready to serve in:** 6 hrs 10 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 6 hrs | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **4** chicken breasts (boneless, skinless)
- [ ] **½ cup** chicken broth
- [ ] **1 tbsp** olive oil
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt
- [ ] **½ tsp** paprika
- [ ] **½ tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **1 tsp** Italian seasoning

## Instructions

### 1. Add broth
Pour **½ cup** chicken broth into the bottom of the slow cooker.

### 2. Add the chicken
Place the **4** chicken breasts in the slow cooker.

### 3. Oil the chicken
Drizzle **1 tbsp** olive oil over the chicken and use a basting brush to rub it in.

### 4. Season
Mix together the salt, paprika, black pepper, and Italian seasoning in a small bowl. Sprinkle over the chicken and rub in with the basting brush.

### 5. Cook
Cover and cook on **LOW for 4–6 hrs** or **HIGH for 2–3 hrs**, until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of **165°F**.

### 6. Serve or shred
Remove from the slow cooker. The chicken is ready to serve whole, or shred it for tacos, salads, sandwiches, and more.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve whole with roasted vegetables or a side salad for a lower-carb plate. Also great shredded over greens for a chicken salad.
- **For the family:** Shred the chicken and use it in white chicken enchiladas, chicken tacos, slow cooker chicken tostadas, buffalo chicken grilled cheese, or chicken Caesar wraps. Also works well whole with mashed potatoes and green beans.

## Storage Tips

- **Refrigerate:** Store in an airtight container for **up to 5 days**.
- **Freeze:** Freeze cooked chicken for **up to 3 months**.
- **Reheat:** Reheat with a splash of broth to keep it moist.

## Notes

- **Cook on LOW for best texture.** The site tested both low and high settings — low produced more tender results and helps prevent dry chicken.
- **Don't overcook.** The chicken can become tough if cooked too long. Use a meat thermometer to check for **165°F**.
- **Use similar-sized breasts** so they all finish cooking at the same time. If one is noticeably larger, add a few extra minutes and check it separately.
- **Let it rest 5 min** before shredding or slicing. This lets the juices redistribute through the meat instead of running out onto the cutting board.
- **Slow cooker size matters.** A **4- to 6-quart** slow cooker works best for 4 chicken breasts. Too large, and the chicken can cook faster than expected and dry out. Too small, and it won't cook evenly.
- **Frozen chicken works:** The site notes you can cook frozen chicken breasts in the crockpot without changing the cooking time — just check with a meat thermometer to ensure **165°F** before serving.
- **Seasoning variations:** Swap in taco seasoning, fajita seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, or fresh herbs like basil, thyme, and rosemary.
- **Other cuts:** This seasoning also works on chicken thighs or chicken tenders.
- **Shredding tip:** Use two forks, or speed things up by shredding with a hand mixer or stand mixer.
- **Reader tip:** One reviewer added a packet of Lipton Onion Soup Mix sprinkled over the top for extra flavor.
