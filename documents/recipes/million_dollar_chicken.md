# Million Dollar Chicken

**Ready to serve in:** 40 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 30 min | **Yields:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **2 lbs** boneless, skinless chicken breasts, thinly cut or pounded
- [ ] **6 oz** cream cheese, softened
- [ ] **¼ cup** mayonnaise
- [ ] **½ cup** crispy bacon, crumbled (about 8 slices)
- [ ] **½ cup** green onions, chopped (about 4 onions)
- [ ] **1½ cups** Colby Jack cheese, shredded

### Garnish

- [ ] Crumbled bacon
- [ ] Green onions

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat and mix
Preheat the oven to **350°F**. In a medium bowl, mix the cream cheese, mayo, bacon crumbles, green onion, and half of the Colby Jack cheese together until combined.

### 2. Assemble
Place the chicken breasts in a baking dish and spread the cheese mixture over the chicken. Top with the remaining cheese.

### 3. Bake
Bake for **30–40 min**, or until the chicken is cooked through (**165°F** internal temperature).

### 4. Serve
Garnish with additional bacon and green onions. Serve hot!

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with roasted veggies, skillet green beans, or a side salad with ranch dressing for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with mashed potatoes, rice, garlic bread, or buttermilk cornbread.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for **3–4 days**, or freeze for **up to 1 month**.
- **Freezer meal prep:** Lay the chicken flat in a freezer-safe dish. Combine the cream cheese, mayo, bacon crumbles, half the Colby Jack, and green onions, then spread over the chicken and freeze (extra cheese and bacon for topping can be frozen separately). Thaw **24–48 hrs** in the refrigerator, add the remaining cheese, and bake as directed. Freezes well for **up to 3 months**.
- **Leftover ideas:**
  - **Grilled sandwiches:** Slice the chicken and place between two slices of good white bread. Spread the creamy topping on one side, add fresh spinach, and grill in a buttered skillet over medium heat until golden.
  - **Stuffed peppers:** Chop leftovers and mix with prepared rice. Stuff into bell pepper halves, top with cheese, and bake covered at 350°F for **25–35 min** until tender and heated through.
  - **Stuffed baked potatoes:** Split baked potatoes, season with salt and pepper, stuff with chopped leftover chicken, top with cheese and bacon, and bake **10–15 min** until melted and heated through.
  - **Rice (or cauli rice) bowls:** Pile reheated chopped chicken over rice or cauliflower rice with spinach, diced red bell pepper, black beans, and green onions. Drizzle with ranch dressing.

## Notes

- **Thin chicken is key:** Use thin-cut chicken breasts, or butterfly/pound them out. Thin breasts cook more quickly and evenly and stay more tender.
- **Doneness:** Chicken breast should reach an internal temperature of **165°F**. Once it goes hotter, it starts to get tough and chewy. A food thermometer is the most reliable way to get it right — otherwise cut into the middle to check.
- **Bake time can vary:** The source says **30–40 min** on the middle rack uncovered at 350°F. One reviewer found it took much longer (up to 90 min) with thicker chicken — thinner is better.
- **Soften the cream cheese** so everything combines well.
- **Bacon:** Can be made ahead and stored in the fridge or freezer, or use precooked bacon. Crisp it back up in the microwave or a skillet before using.
- **Substitutions:**
  - **Chicken:** Boneless, skinless thighs or tenderloins work too.
  - **Cream cheese:** Light cream cheese works.
  - **Colby Jack:** Cheddar or Mexican blend works.
  - **Egg-free:** Use sour cream or vegan mayo in place of the mayonnaise.
- **Variations:**
  - **Skinny version:** Use light cream cheese, light mayo, and reduce the Colby Jack mixed in by **½ cup**.
  - **Ranch version:** Mix **1 tbsp** ranch seasoning into the cream cheese mixture.
  - **Spinach version:** Mix **12 oz** frozen spinach (thawed, with excess moisture squeezed out) into the cream cheese mixture.
  - **With potatoes:** Use a larger baking dish and arrange halved baby red or gold potatoes (tossed in olive oil, salt, and garlic powder) around the chicken. Must be small potatoes so they cook at the same rate.
- **Dietary notes:** Naturally gluten-free, nut-free, low-carb (**2g net carbs** per serving), and keto-friendly.
- **Bacon shortcut (reader tip):** Bake bacon on a parchment-lined cookie sheet at **350°F for 18–23 min** while you prep the chicken and creamy mixture.
