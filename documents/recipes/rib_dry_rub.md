# Rib Dry Rub

**Ready to serve in:** 5 min

**Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 0 min | **Yields:** 10 servings (enough for 1–3 racks of ribs)

## Ingredients

- [ ] **¼ cup** brown sugar
- [ ] **2 tsp** kosher salt
- [ ] **2 tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **2 tsp** smoked paprika
- [ ] **1 tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **1 tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **1 tsp** ground mustard powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** ground cinnamon
- [ ] **½ tsp** celery salt
- [ ] **¼ tsp** cayenne pepper

## Instructions

### 1. Make the rub
Mix all ingredients in a bowl. Use a fork to crush any clumps of sugar or seasonings.

### 2. Use or store
Store in an airtight container for **up to 1 month**.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Use it on your favorite rib recipe — including the **Oven Baked Ribs** already in this book.
- **For the family:** Works on smoked, grilled, or oven-baked ribs. Also great on pork chops, chicken, or any BBQ protein.

## Storage Tips

- Store in an airtight glass container for **at least a month**. You may need to shake or mix to break up brown sugar clumps before using.

## Notes

- **How much does this cover?** The recipe makes enough for **1–3 racks** of ribs, depending on how heavily you season. If you love a lot of bark, make plenty and season heavy. For 5–6 racks, triple the batch.
- **Why these ingredients:**
  - **Brown sugar** is the base — pork and sweet go together.
  - **Smoked paprika** adds wood-fired flavor and a gorgeous red color.
  - **Onion and garlic** are classic flavor enhancers.
  - **Ground mustard** adds a punchy, almost acidic brightness — it ends up in almost all of this pitmaster's rubs.
  - **Cinnamon** is a secret ingredient — it adds a lip-smacking quality people can't identify but keep coming back for.
  - **Celery salt** is the earthy balance to the sweet brown sugar. Balance is crucial in a good dry rub.
- **Adjustments:**
  - For less heat (or for kids), cut the black pepper in half.
  - Not a fan of smoked paprika? Sweet paprika works — the pellet grill will still add smokiness.
- **Versatility:** This rub is fantastic on its own — you don't even need sauce. It's also great on pork chops, chicken, and other BBQ proteins.
- **Bottled version:** Hey Grill Hey sells this rub pre-made if you'd rather skip the mixing.
