# Sticky BBQ Ribs

**Ready to serve in:** 1 hr 10 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 1 hr | **Yields:** 4–6 servings (about 3 lbs ribs)

## Ingredients

- [ ] **3 lbs** pork back ribs
- [ ] **1 (8-oz) jar** honey
- [ ] **1 tsp** paprika
- [ ] **1 tsp** chili powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **2 tbsp** Old Bay Seasoning
- [ ] **½ tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **¼ tsp** celery salt
- [ ] **½ cup** dark brown sugar
- [ ] **¼ tsp** freshly ground pepper
- [ ] **1 medium** onion, grated or finely chopped
- [ ] **12 oz** barbecue sauce
- [ ] **¼ cup** white sugar

## Instructions

### 1. Combine everything in the pan
Place all ingredients together in a large roasting pan (the bottom of a turkey roaster works well).

### 2. Cut the ribs
Cut the ribs apart for easier serving.

### 3. Coat the ribs
Mix everything together, making sure to coat all the ribs with this semi-dry paste.

### 4. Arrange and cover
Spread the ribs out evenly on the bottom of the pan. Cover lightly with foil.

### 5. Bake
Bake at **375°F** for approximately **1 hr**, turning or stirring occasionally.

### 6. Serve
These ribs are also great finished on the grill for a few minutes on each side, or under the broiler — just watch more closely and adjust the cooking time.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with a green salad or roasted vegetables for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with baked potatoes, baked squash, or cornbread, and a green salad on the side.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for **up to 4 days**.
- Reheat gently in the oven, covered, until warmed through.
- Freezes well for **up to 3 months**.

## Nutrition

- **Sweet & sticky** — honey, brown sugar, and white sugar create a glossy, caramelized coating.
- **Bold flavor** — Old Bay, chili powder, and paprika give it a savory-spicy backbone.
- **One-pan** — everything goes into the roasting pan together, so cleanup is easy.

## Notes

- **A note from the author:** This is a "dump everything in the pan" recipe. The original instructions say to "place all ingredients together in a large roasting pan," which some readers found confusing — they thought the BBQ sauce should go on at the end. It doesn't; everything goes in together. The sauce cooks down into a sticky glaze.
- **Reader favorite tweak — lower and slower:** Many reviewers preferred baking at **325°F for about 3 hrs**. This lets the flavors permeate the meat more and produces fall-off-the-bone ribs.
- **Cut the sweetness:** Several readers found it too sweet. Common fixes:
  - Replace the **¼ cup white sugar** with **¼ cup vinegar** to cut the sweetness.
  - Omit the white sugar entirely and use honey BBQ sauce instead of adding honey.
  - Reduce the brown sugar slightly.
- **Old Bay substitute:** If you can't find Old Bay (one reader in Canada couldn't), you can make your own or use **1 tbsp** of a similar seasoned salt blend.
- **Other protein:** One reader doesn't eat pork and made the sauce with two whole chickens, cut into 8 portions each — 1 hr covered, then 30 min uncovered, basting every 10 min. Reportedly excellent, and even better cold the next day.
- **Finish on the grill:** These are also great finished on the grill for a few minutes per side, or under a preheated broiler for 5–10 min to add a bit of "mouth feel" similar to grilled ribs.
- **Reader swaps that work:** Lyell's Golden Syrup in place of honey, Asian chili-garlic sauce in place of chili powder, kosher salt instead of celery salt, and a dry rub applied first before adding the sauce mixture.
