# Chimeran
## Classification
- An individual becomes a Chimeran after surviving the chimera serum
- Classification is automatic upon survival

## The Serum
- Human-animal hybridization via engineered virus
- Created from a theorem; after a few iterations, one successful test subject
- Purpose: soldiers with elevated advantages over enemies
- Numbered variants, each tuned to a different species
- First success: Osyra Renaya — Chimera-9, wolf variant

## Successful Hosts
- Present animal-like traits and appendages (animal ears, animal tails)
- Red irises always left behind as the marker of infection
- Intelligence retained

## Outcomes
- Most participants did not survive
- A few did not die but did not retain humanity — feral, animalistic
- No super strength, no healing factor, no speed, no combat powers
- Enhancements are sensory and psychological only

## Variants
- Numbered (e.g. Chimera-9 = wolf)
- Each tuned to a different species
- Program continues; other Chimerans appear over time

## Social Status
- Not accepted in society
- Must hide animal traits when in public

## Concealment
- Colored contacts (to hide red irises)
- Hats (to hide animal ears)
- Baggy clothing (to hide tail appendages)
- Teeth alterations (some variants); they don't smile or open their mouths


# Aurora Pulse
## Basic
- Nightclub owned by Uta Yuki; she occasionally DJs
- Converted factory warehouse
- Located on city edge
- Hours: 8PM – 4AM

## Layout

### Dance Floor
- Black lights, neon, warped lines (disorienting by design)
- Perimeter seating ledge

### Stage
- Karaoke front, DJ booth in corner
- Booth has sliding wall to lock equipment

### Sound
- Ceiling speakers on poles, all directions
- Loud enough to feel physically

### Lighting
- UV/black light reactive to music
- Source of club name

### Entrance
- Front-right; ticket booth controls door (exit free, entry locked)

### Bathrooms
- Dim (drug deterrent)

### Games Room (right)
- Pool, foosball, regular lighting
- Double-pane glass blocks music bleed

### Kitchen & Bar (left)
- Colored lighting; engineered to not affect dance floor

### Back Hallway
- Pantries, freezer, Uta's office, security office, staff room
- Only other exit (staff door)

### VIP Booth
- Above ticket booth, tinted window, view of floor

### Exterior
- Full mural: Aurora Borealis on warehouse shell
- Benches out front (unofficial hangout)

### Parking
- Flat concrete lot

## Admission
- 21+ rule
- Wristband grants entry and key for restricted areas
- Aurora Pulse has an app
- ID scanned; checksum written to band; face match at door
- There's different emblems for different tiers. They glow in black light. 
- Lifetime membership costs about 10 years of year passes
- Passes expire 6 AM each day (after closing)

## Security
- Ticket booth attendant armed; leaving booth = building lockdown
- Bouncers pose as floor staff; pepper spray/taser
- Hidden lockboxes with real weapons
- Cameras on springs (bass-proof); dedicated watcher
- Pat downs, and bag scanners, no honor system + bouncers

## Order Discs
- Vibrating pucks for orders in loud room
- Color-coded: 🟢 food, 🔵 bar, 🔴 security alert
- If there's a security alert, all of the pucks in the building will start vibrating and turn red. 

## Staff
- Waiters, bar, kitchen, bouncers, camera watcher
- Staff room with changing/makeup area

## Menu
- Adult bar menu,
- Common greasy bar foods usually deep fried. 

## Music
- Builds through night: chill → hype
- Genres: house, techno, funk, phonk
- Productivity radio before open/after close

## Dress Code
- No weapons
- No face coverings at door
- Shoes required
- Nothing blatantly offensive

## Merch
- Small display near entrance; mostly sold online

## Chimeran Policy
- Uta knows about Chimerans; doesn't ask, doesn't treat differently
