# Pork Stir-Fry

**Ready to serve in:** 20 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 10 min | **Yields:** 3–4 servings

## Ingredients

### For the Pork

- [ ] **1 lb** pork (loin, tenderloin, or shoulder – slice thin against the grain)
- [ ] **1 tbsp** low-sodium soy sauce (or tamari)
- [ ] **1 tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **1 tbsp** olive or avocado oil (for cooking)

### For the Sauce

- [ ] **¼ cup** low-sodium chicken broth (or water)
- [ ] **3 tbsp** low-sodium soy sauce
- [ ] **1 tbsp** rice vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)
- [ ] **1 tsp** grated fresh ginger (or **½ tsp** ground ginger)
- [ ] **2 cloves** garlic, minced (or **½ tsp** garlic powder)
- [ ] *Optional:* **½ tsp** xanthan gum or **1 tsp** cornstarch mixed with **2 tbsp** water (to thicken – skip if you prefer a thinner sauce)

### For the Veggies

- [ ] **1 bag** (14 oz) coleslaw mix (shredded cabbage + carrots) – or use sliced bell peppers, broccoli florets, and onions
- [ ] **2 tbsp** olive or avocado oil (for stir-frying)
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

### Optional Garnish

- [ ] Sliced green onions
- [ ] Sesame seeds
- [ ] Sriracha or red pepper flakes (for heat)

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the pork
Slice the pork as thin as possible (**¼ inch or less**) against the grain – this keeps it tender. Toss with **1 tbsp soy sauce**, garlic powder, and black pepper. Let it sit for **5–10 min** while you prep the sauce.

### 2. Make the sauce
In a small bowl, whisk together chicken broth, **3 tbsp soy sauce**, vinegar, ginger, and garlic. (If using xanthan gum or cornstarch thickener, whisk it in now.)

### 3. Cook the pork
Heat **1 tbsp oil** in a large skillet or wok over **high heat**. Add pork in a single layer (cook in batches if needed – don't overcrowd!). Cook for **2–3 minutes per side**, until browned and cooked through. Remove to a plate.

### 4. Stir-fry the veggies
Add **2 tbsp oil** to the same skillet. Add the coleslaw mix (or your chopped veggies) and stir-fry over high heat for **3–4 minutes**, until crisp-tender and slightly wilted. Sprinkle with a pinch of salt.

### 5. Combine
Return the pork to the pan. Pour the sauce over everything. Toss and stir-fry for **1–2 minutes** until the sauce thickens slightly (if using thickener) and coats everything evenly.

### 6. Finish
Remove from heat. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds if you have them. Add a drizzle of sriracha if you like heat.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve over cauliflower rice for a low-carb meal.
- **For the family:** Pair with steamed jasmine rice or noodles.

## Nutrition

- **High-protein** – pork provides a lean, satisfying base.
- **Low-carb friendly** – carbs come only from the veggies (~**8–10g** per serving).
- **Quick & easy** – ready in just 20 minutes!
