# Beef Burritos

**Ready to serve in:** 35 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Yields:** 6–8 burritos

## Ingredients

### Burrito Seasoning

- [ ] **1 tsp each** onion powder, dried oregano, salt
- [ ] **2 tsp each** dried cumin powder, paprika
- [ ] **¼ tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **¼ tsp** cayenne pepper, or to taste *(optional)*

### Beef Filling

- [ ] **½ tbsp** olive oil
- [ ] **2 cloves** garlic
- [ ] **½** onion, finely chopped
- [ ] **500 g / 1 lb** beef mince (ground beef), lean
- [ ] **2 tbsp** tomato paste
- [ ] **3 tbsp** water

### Burritos

- [ ] **6–8 large** soft flour tortillas or round wraps (~25 cm / 10"+) 
- [ ] **3 cups** cooked rice, warm but not piping hot (white long grain works well)
- [ ] **3 cups** iceberg lettuce or cabbage, finely sliced (use cabbage if freezing)
- [ ] **1 cup** corn kernels (canned, drained)
- [ ] **1 cup** black beans (canned, drained)
- [ ] **3** tomatoes, deseeded and diced
- [ ] **½** red onion, finely chopped
- [ ] Finely chopped coriander / cilantro *(optional)*
- [ ] **1½ cups / 150 g** shredded cheese of choice (melting kind)

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the beef filling
Heat oil in a skillet over **high heat**. Add garlic and onion, cook for **2 min** until the onion is lightly browned. Add beef and cook, breaking it up as you go, until it turns from red to brown.

### 2. Season
Add the burrito seasoning (or 1 packet store-bought taco seasoning) and cook for **1 min**. Then add the tomato paste and water, and cook for **3 min** until the water is mostly evaporated but the beef is still juicy, not dried out. Set aside to cool for **10 min** — piping hot filling will steam the burrito and make the tortilla soggy.

### 3. Warm the tortillas
*Optional:* If not warming the rolled burrito afterward, warm the tortillas before rolling (per packet directions).

### 4. Fill
Place a burrito on a work surface. Place **¼–⅓ cup** of rice just below the center. Top with **¼–⅓ cup** beef, then some lettuce, corn, black beans, tomato, a sprinkle of red onion, coriander, and cheese.

### 5. Roll
Fold up the bottom to cover the filling, then fold the edges in. Roll up tightly, then wrap in foil. Serve as-is or heat per below.

### 6. Heat through *(optional)*
To warm through, pan fry (dry pan) in the foil over **medium-high heat** for a few minutes on each side to slightly crisp and warm the outside. Or for **10+ min** on medium-low heat if you want to melt the cheese. They can also be baked at **180°C / 350°F for 20 min** in the foil — this warms them all the way through and crisps the tortilla slightly. If they're straight from the fridge, they'll take around **30 min** to warm through.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Skip the rice to lower the carbs, or use cabbage instead of lettuce for extra crunch. Add extra veggies into the beef filling (see Notes).
- **For the family:** Serve as-is, with sour cream or yogurt and a chili sauce for dipping. Restaurant-style salsa or guacamole are great if you want to make more of an effort.

## Storage Tips

- **Freeze:** Wrap in cling wrap, then place in a ziplock bag or airtight container. Freeze well as long as you avoid watery vegetables — use cabbage instead of lettuce, and don't add avocado or cucumber.
- **Thaw:** Thaw for **24 hrs** in the fridge, or overnight on the counter (as long as it's not super hot where you are).
- **Reheat:**
  - **Oven:** Reheat in foil at **180°C / 350°F for 30–40 min** (place on a tray in case of leakage) until the outside is slightly crisp.
  - **Microwave + stovetop:** Unwrap, microwave for **2 min**, then crisp in a dry pan over medium heat.
  - **Stovetop:** Leave in foil and turn over medium heat in a dry pan for **10+ min** until crisp all over (takes a while to warm all the way through).

## Notes

- **Tortillas:** Mission large soft wraps work well — slightly larger and softer than standard burrito wraps, so less prone to breaking.
- **Rice note:** **1 cup** uncooked rice yields about **3 cups** cooked. Don't make the mistake of cooking 3 cups uncooked — that's way too much.
- **Freezer filling tips:**
  - Use ingredients that freeze well.
  - Use cabbage instead of lettuce — cabbage holds up to freezing better.
  - Use only a small amount of "watery" vegetables like tomato — they become even more watery once defrosted.
  - Avoid avocado and cucumber, which don't freeze well.
  - Cooked vegetables like capsicum/peppers, zucchini, and carrot can be diced and added to the beef filling.
- **More veggies / lower carb:** To pack in more veggies (and/or reduce carbs by skipping the rice), add veggies into the beef filling. Increase the seasoning, tomato paste, and water by **50%**, then add **3 cups** diced veggies (zucchini, carrots, celery, capsicum, corn). Use a large skillet, sauté the veggies after the onion, push to the side, then add the beef and proceed.
- **Double the seasoning:** Several readers double the spice mix so they have plenty — great idea if you make these often.
- **Cheese amount:** One reader found **1½ cups** cheese spread thin across 8 burritos and recommended doubling it.
- **Wrap trick:** Wrap in baking paper before the foil — the burrito won't stick, and it's easier to unwrap and keep rolled tight for the microwave.
- **Reader swap:** Coleslaw mix works great in place of the cabbage.
