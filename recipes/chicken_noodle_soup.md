# Creamy Chicken Noodle Soup

**Ready to serve in:** 3 hrs 40 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 3 hrs 30 min | **Yields:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **3 stalks** celery, diced
- [ ] **2 medium** carrots, peeled and sliced
- [ ] **1 lb** chicken breasts, boneless skinless, whole and raw
- [ ] **½ tbsp** dried thyme
- [ ] **½ tbsp** dried basil
- [ ] **2 cloves** garlic, minced
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt
- [ ] **½ tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **6 cups** chicken stock
- [ ] **3 tbsp** unsalted butter
- [ ] **⅓ cup** all-purpose flour
- [ ] **1½ cups** dried penne noodles
- [ ] **1 cup** heavy cream

## Instructions

### 1. Start the soup
Combine the diced celery, carrots, whole chicken breasts, chicken stock, thyme, basil, garlic, salt, and pepper in the slow cooker. Cook on **LOW for 6 hrs** or on **HIGH for 3 hrs**.

### 2. Make the roux
After 3 hrs (on high) or 6 hrs (on low), pull **2 cups** of liquid from the slow cooker and set aside. Melt the butter in a small saucepan. Whisk in the flour until smooth. Slowly add the reserved liquid, starting with about **2 tbsp** at a time (no need to measure) and adding more once each addition is fully whisked in. This should take you more than **30 seconds** but less than **2 min**.

### 3. Thicken the soup
Add the roux to the slow cooker and stir to combine.

### 4. Add the noodles
Add the uncooked penne noodles and turn the slow cooker to **HIGH**. Cook for **30 min**.

### 5. Shred the chicken
Turn off the slow cooker, remove the chicken breasts, and shred them. Return the shredded chicken to the slow cooker.

### 6. Finish
Stir in the heavy cream. Serve and enjoy.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve on its own as a hearty one-pot meal, or with a simple arugula or kale salad on the side.
- **For the family:** Serve with beer bread, dinner rolls, or cheddar drop biscuits for dipping.

## Storage Tips

- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for **up to 4 days**.
- The noodles will continue to absorb liquid as it sits — still delicious, but thicker than when first served. Reheat over **low heat** to keep the creamy consistency.
- **Freezing:** Not ideal because of the noodles. If you want to freeze it, leave the noodles out and add freshly cooked noodles when reheating.

## Nutrition

- **Comforting & creamy** – the roux and heavy cream give it a rich, thick body.
- **High-protein** – whole chicken breasts cook right in the broth, then get shredded back in.
- **Flexible** – easy to lighten up or make gluten-free (see Notes).

## Notes

- **Skip the cream:** For a traditional (non-creamy) chicken noodle soup, replace the heavy cream with **1 more cup** of chicken stock and skip the roux entirely.
- **Roux tips:** Let the butter melt completely before whisking in the flour. The mixture will be very thick at first. Add the liquid slowly — it should end up a nice, creamy, pourable liquid. If it's too thick, add a little more stock.
- **Pasta in the slow cooker:** Stir the noodles in well and cook for **30 min on HIGH**. Works with any noodle type.
- **Variations:**
  - Add a diced yellow onion for extra flavor.
  - Swap the basil for oregano or parsley if you're allergic or prefer a different herb.
  - Use half-and-half instead of heavy cream to lighten it up.
  - Try egg noodles, elbow macaroni, or rotini instead of penne. (One reader used gnocchi and loved it.)
- **Gluten-free:** Use gluten-free noodles and swap the flour for almond flour. The roux will be slightly less creamy but still works.
- **Frozen chicken:** You can add it frozen, but you'll need to increase the cooking time.
- **Rotisserie chicken:** Works, but add it at the very end just to warm through — don't cook it in the slow cooker from the start.
