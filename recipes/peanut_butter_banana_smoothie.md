# Peanut Butter Banana Smoothie

**Ready to serve in:** 5 min

**Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 0 min | **Yields:** 1 smoothie (about 16 oz without ice)

## Ingredients

- [ ] **¾ cup** unsweetened almond milk
- [ ] **1 large** banana, cut into chunks and frozen
- [ ] **2 tbsp** creamy peanut butter
- [ ] **½ cup** nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- [ ] **¼ tsp** ground cinnamon
- [ ] Ice *(optional)*

### Optional Mix-ins

- [ ] **½ scoop** protein powder (vanilla or chocolate)
- [ ] **1 tbsp** flaxseed meal
- [ ] **1 tbsp** chia seeds

## Instructions

### 1. Combine
Place all of the ingredients in a blender in the order listed: almond milk, banana, peanut butter, Greek yogurt, cinnamon, and any extra mix-ins.

### 2. Blend
Blend until smooth. If you'd like the smoothie thicker, add a few ice cubes and blend again. Pour and enjoy!

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Enjoy as a filling, protein-rich breakfast on its own, or as a post-workout shake.
- **For the family:** A crowd-pleaser for kids — tastes like a milkshake but is good for you. Also works as a smoothie bowl (see Notes).

## Storage Tips

- **Refrigerate:** Store in airtight containers or jars for **up to 1 day**.
- **Freeze:** Freeze in freezer-safe jars or ice cube trays for **up to 3 months**. Let jars thaw overnight in the refrigerator before serving. If using an ice cube tray, toss the frozen cubes into the blender for a smoothie on demand.

## Notes

- **Frozen banana is key:** It makes the smoothie plenty sweet on its own and gives it that thick, milkshake-like texture. If you'd like it sweeter, add a few teaspoons of honey or pure maple syrup.
- **Milk swap:** Unsweetened almond milk keeps the smoothie from being too sweet, but any milk — dairy or non-dairy — works.
- **Blender tip:** If you're not using a high-powered blender, you may need to thin the smoothie with more almond milk to get it to blend properly. Add the frozen banana in stages, blending between each.
- **If it's too thin:** Add another banana or a handful of ice. One reader found the base recipe liquidy and fixed it with an extra banana.
- **Variations:**
  - **Chocolate:** Add a few teaspoons of cocoa powder or a scoop of chocolate protein powder.
  - **Protein boost:** Add **½–1 scoop** of your favorite protein powder (vanilla or chocolate).
  - **Smoothie bowl:** Add extra banana or ice to thicken further, then serve in a bowl with toppings like granola, banana slices, blueberries, and a drizzle of peanut butter. Start with **½ cup** ice.
  - **Omega-3 boost:** Add **1 tbsp** chia seeds or flaxseed meal.
- **Picky eater approved:** This one passes the kid test — it tastes like a treat without being overtly "healthy."
- **Banana-saving tip:** When bananas get too ripe, chop and freeze them so you always have some ready for this smoothie.
