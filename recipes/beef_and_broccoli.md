# Beef and Broccoli

**Ready to serve in:** 30 min

**Prep:** 17 min | **Cook:** 13 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

### For the Stir Fry

- [ ] **1 lb** flank steak, very thinly sliced into bite-sized strips
- [ ] **2 tbsp** olive oil (or vegetable oil), divided
- [ ] **1 lb** fresh broccoli, cut into **6 cups** of florets
- [ ] **2 tsp** sesame seeds, for garnish *(optional)*

### For the Stir Fry Sauce

- [ ] **1 tsp** fresh ginger, grated (loosely packed)
- [ ] **2 tsp** garlic, grated (from about 3 cloves)
- [ ] **½ cup** hot water
- [ ] **6 tbsp** low-sodium soy sauce (or GF tamari)
- [ ] **3 tbsp** packed light brown sugar
- [ ] **1½ tbsp** cornstarch
- [ ] **¼ tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **2 tbsp** sesame oil

## Instructions

### 1. Prep
Start cooking white rice first so it's ready when the stir fry is done. Cover and freeze the steak for **30 min** for easier slicing, then thinly slice against the grain.

### 2. Make the sauce
Combine all stir fry sauce ingredients in a bowl, stir well to dissolve the sugar, and set aside.

### 3. Cook the broccoli
Place a large skillet over **medium heat** and add **1 tbsp** oil. Add the broccoli florets and sauté for **4–5 min**, partially covered with a lid, stirring or tossing several times until the broccoli is bright green and crisp-tender. Remove from the pan. *(Tip: For softer broccoli, add **2 tbsp** water before covering with the lid — it will steam-cook the broccoli.)*

### 4. Cook the beef
Increase heat to **high** and add the remaining **1 tbsp** oil. Add the beef in a single layer and sauté **2 min per side**, or just until cooked through. Quickly pull out a piece to test for doneness.

### 5. Finish
Add the sauce and reduce heat to **medium-low**. Simmer **3–4 min** — it will thicken. Add the broccoli back and stir to combine. Stir in **1–2 tbsp** water to thin the sauce if desired. Serve over white rice.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve over cauliflower rice or on its own for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve over steamed white rice, or add cooked noodles for a Beef Broccoli Ramen Stir Fry.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate once cooled to room temperature for **3–4 days**. For meal prep, store in individual containers to reheat portions.
- **Freezing:** Possible, but the texture will suffer — broccoli can get mushy when thawed.
- **Reheat:** Set portions in a skillet and stir fry over **medium heat** until hot. Add a splash of water to loosen up the sauce.
- **Prep ahead:** Slice the beef, prepare the broccoli, and make the sauce the day before. Store them all separately until ready to cook.

## Notes

- **Best cut of beef:** Flank steak is ideal — tender, lean, and easy to chew. Other options: top sirloin, top round, tri-tip, or ribeye (for extra flavor and richness).
- **Slice it thin:** Freezing the steak for about **30 min** firms it up so you can slice thin and even. Slice against the grain, between **⅛" and ¼"** thick — the thinner the better. Thinner slices cook faster, stay tender, and absorb the sauce better.
- **Fresh broccoli only.** Frozen broccoli gets mushy, releases liquid, and dilutes the sauce, making it bland.
- **Sauce tips:**
  - Use low-sodium soy sauce (or GF tamari) so you can adjust to taste.
  - Don't overdo the fresh ginger — it has a strong flavor.
  - Don't skip the sesame oil — it gives authentic flavor.
  - Simmer the sauce for at least **3 min** to mellow out the garlic and ginger.
  - For a thinner sauce, add **2 tbsp** water at the end.
- **Hot pan matters:** Add the steak to a hot skillet to sear and seal in the juices.
- **Don't overcrowd the pan** — it cools the pan and results in steamed, not seared, beef.
- **Veggie variations:** Snap peas, bell peppers, carrots, and mushrooms all work. Use fresh, not frozen.
- **Reader tip:** One reviewer wished they'd salted and peppered the beef while it cooked — the sauce didn't fully seep into the meat. Season the beef during cooking if you want more flavor in every bite.
- **Gluten-free:** Use GF tamari in place of soy sauce. One reader confirms it works great.
