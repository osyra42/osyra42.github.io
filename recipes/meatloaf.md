# Meatloaf

**Ready to serve in:** 1 hr 20 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 1 hr 5 min | **Yields:** 8 servings

## Ingredients

### For the Meatloaf

- [ ] **2 lbs** ground beef (85% or 80% lean)
- [ ] **1 medium** onion (about **1 cup**), finely chopped
- [ ] **1 tsp** olive oil
- [ ] **2 large** eggs
- [ ] **3 cloves** garlic, minced
- [ ] **2 tbsp** ketchup
- [ ] **3 tbsp** fresh parsley, finely chopped
- [ ] **¾ cup** panko breadcrumbs (or gluten-free breadcrumbs)
- [ ] **⅓ cup** milk
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt, or to taste
- [ ] **1 tsp** Italian seasoning
- [ ] **½ tsp** ground black pepper

### For the Glaze

- [ ] **¾ cup** ketchup
- [ ] **1½ tsp** white vinegar
- [ ] **2 tbsp** brown sugar
- [ ] **½ tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** onion powder

## Instructions

### 1. Prep
Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper or foil for easier cleanup. Preheat oven to **350°F**.

### 2. Sauté the onions
Set a medium skillet over **medium heat**. Add **1 tsp** oil and the chopped onion, and sauté, stirring occasionally, until softened and golden, about **5–7 min**. Transfer to a plate to cool.

### 3. Make the meatloaf mixture
In a large bowl, add all of the meatloaf ingredients plus the cooled onions. Mix **just until well combined** — your hands are the best tool for this (put on disposable gloves if you want).

### 4. Shape and bake
Add the meat to the prepared pan and shape it into a loaf about **8" long, 4" wide, and 3" tall**. Bake uncovered at **350°F** for **40 min**.

### 5. Make the glaze
In a small bowl, stir together all the sauce ingredients until combined.

### 6. Glaze and finish baking
Spread the sauce over the meatloaf, then return to the oven and bake an additional **20 min**, or until the internal temperature reaches **160°F** on an instant-read thermometer. Rest the meatloaf for **10–15 min** before serving — it will be much easier to slice.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with roasted broccoli, roasted cauliflower, or a Caesar salad for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with creamy mashed potatoes, baked potatoes, or roasted potatoes. Green beans, roasted carrots, baked mac and cheese, or buttered pasta all work well too.

## Storage Tips

- **Assemble ahead:** Assemble 1–2 days before baking. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate until ready to cook.
- **Refrigerate leftovers:** Store cooled meatloaf in an airtight container for **3–4 days**. Great for meatloaf sandwiches, tacos, or burritos.
- **Freeze raw:** Shape the meatloaf free-form or in a loaf pan, cover with plastic wrap and aluminum foil, and freeze for **up to 6 months**. Defrost covered in the refrigerator for **24 hrs**, then add **5 min** to the baking time.
- **Freeze cooked:** Once cooled, freeze portions in freezer-safe zip bags for **up to 3 months**. Thaw in the refrigerator overnight, then reheat in the oven or air fryer.

## Notes

- **Don't overmix.** This is the secret to keeping meatloaf moist — overworking the meat makes it tough. Mix just until combined.
- **Sauté the onions.** This quick step tones down harsh onion flavor (and helps prevent "onion burps"), and the onions blend into the meatloaf better with a softer texture.
- **Bake free-form.** Shaping on a baking sheet instead of a loaf pan gives the best crust and more airflow, which means less grey residue buildup (coagulated fats and proteins). If you see residue, spoon or dab it off with a paper towel.
- **Rest before slicing.** **10–15 min** lets the juices redistribute and makes slicing much easier.
- **Meat variations:** Replace half the ground beef with ground turkey, chicken, pork, or Italian sausage. Note: if using poultry, bake to **165°F** instead of 160°F.
- **Doneness:** The most reliable way to check is an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part. Remove at **160°F** (or **165°F** for poultry). Different ovens and pans can shift the bake time.
- **Substitutions:**
  - **Breadcrumbs:** Italian breadcrumbs or gluten-free breadcrumbs work in place of panko.
  - **Ketchup (in the meatloaf):** Can be replaced with **1 tbsp** Worcestershire sauce.
  - **Glaze:** In a pinch, use your favorite BBQ sauce instead of the homemade glaze.
- **Loaf pan version:** Line the pan with parchment for easier removal. Gently press the meat mixture in (don't pack it down firmly). Bake following the same instructions, but increase the temperature to **375°F** — otherwise it takes longer to cook through.
- **Muffin tin version (reader tip):** Bake for **25 min**, then **10 min** with the sauce. Smaller size means faster cooking, and it's a hit with kids.
- **If it's not done at the one-hour mark:** Make sure you're using the correct loaf pan size and that your oven is fully preheated. Letting the oven preheat **10–15 min past the beep** helps ensure it's truly at temperature.
