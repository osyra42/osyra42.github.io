# Sausage Tortellini Soup

**Ready to serve in:** 40 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 30 min | **Yields:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **16 oz** Italian sausage (mild, or hot for spice)
- [ ] **½ medium** onion, chopped finely
- [ ] **2 sticks** celery, chopped finely
- [ ] **2 tbsp** flour
- [ ] **3–4 cloves** garlic, minced
- [ ] **4 cups** chicken broth
- [ ] **1 (28-oz) can** diced tomatoes, with juices
- [ ] **¼ tsp** Italian seasoning
- [ ] **2 generous cups** refrigerated cheese tortellini
- [ ] **1 cup** heavy whipping cream
- [ ] **2 cups** (packed) fresh baby spinach *(optional)*
- [ ] Salt & pepper to taste

## Instructions

### 1. Brown the sausage
Add the sausage meat to a large soup pot. Sauté over **medium-high heat**, stirring occasionally, for **7–10 min**, or until browned all over.

### 2. Set aside and drain
Take the sausage out of the pot and set it aside on a paper towel-lined plate. Leave about **1 tbsp** of the fat in the pot (drain any excess).

### 3. Sauté the aromatics
Add the onion and celery to the pot and cook for **4–5 min**.

### 4. Add flour and garlic
Add the flour and garlic and cook for **1 min**, stirring constantly.

### 5. Add the broth
Slowly add the chicken broth and stir until the flour has completely dissolved.

### 6. Add tomatoes and sausage
Add the canned tomatoes, Italian seasoning, and the sausage back to the pot. Increase the heat to **high** and bring the soup to a gentle boil.

### 7. Cook the tortellini
Once boiling, add the tortellini and reduce the heat so it's gently simmering. Cook for **5 min**.

### 8. Add cream
Stir in the cream and cook for another **5 min**.

### 9. Finish
Stir in the spinach and season with salt & pepper to taste.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve on its own or with a simple side salad (like a Parmesan arugula salad) for a hearty low-carb-friendly meal. Skip the bread.
- **For the family:** Serve with a slice of garlic bread and a 10-minute homemade Caesar salad on the side. Shaved fresh Parmesan on top takes it over the top.

## Storage Tips

- Best eaten within **a couple of days** — the tortellini will soak up the broth and puff up the longer it sits in the fridge.
- **Make-ahead tip:** If you plan on having leftovers, add the tortellini to individual portions when you reheat them instead. That way the pasta cooks fresh in the soup and won't go too soft.
- **Freezing:** You can freeze this soup, but if freezing the entire batch, leave out the cream, spinach, and tortellini and add them when reheating. If you freeze leftovers as-is, the pasta may not have the same texture as when first cooked.

## Nutrition

- **Comforting & hearty** – Italian sausage and tortellini make this a filling meal on its own.
- **Creamy tomato broth** – flour thickens the broth, and heavy cream gives it a silky finish.
- **Flexible** – easy to swap sausage types, tomatoes, or greens (see Notes).

## Notes

- **Sausage:** Anything around **16 oz / 1 lb** works — it doesn't need to be exact. You can buy ground sausage meat, or take whole sausages out of their casings.
- **Don't skip the cream.** The acidity of the tomatoes may curdle anything with a lower fat content than heavy cream, so be careful with substitutions.
- **Tortellini:** Refrigerated tortellini cooks faster; dried works but takes longer.
- **Variations:**
  - Swap Italian sausage for chicken or turkey sausage.
  - Use petite diced tomatoes or tomato sauce if you don't like regular diced tomatoes.
  - Add crushed red pepper flakes or use hot Italian sausage for heat.
  - Swap spinach for kale — let it cook a little longer.
  - This recipe is very flexible — add in more veggies if you have any that need using up. One reader added green pepper and garbanzo beans with great results.
