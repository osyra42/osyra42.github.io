# Ground Turkey Pasta

**Ready to serve in:** 30 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 15 min | **Yields:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **12 oz** medium shell pasta (or pasta of choice)
- [ ] **1 lb (16 oz)** ground turkey
- [ ] **3 tbsp** olive oil
- [ ] **2 cloves** garlic, minced
- [ ] **½ large** red bell pepper, diced small
- [ ] **4 oz** white mushrooms, thinly sliced or diced small
- [ ] **½ large** white onion, diced
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt (taste and add more as desired)
- [ ] **½ tsp** pepper
- [ ] **1 tsp** Italian seasoning
- [ ] **2 tbsp** all-purpose flour
- [ ] **2 cups** beef broth (or chicken broth)
- [ ] **1 (15-oz) can** tomato sauce
- [ ] **1 cup** heavy cream
- [ ] **½ cup** Parmesan cheese, freshly grated
- [ ] Fresh basil, for garnish *(optional)*

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the pasta
Boil the water for the pasta and follow the directions on the shell pasta box. Typically **8–10 min** to cook medium shells.

### 2. Brown the turkey
In a Dutch oven or wide stock pot, heat **2 tbsp** of the olive oil over **medium heat** and cook the ground turkey. Season with salt and pepper. Once cooked, drain excess fat/liquid and transfer the turkey to a bowl; set aside.

### 3. Sauté the veggies
Add the remaining **1 tbsp** olive oil to the same pan. Sauté the garlic, bell peppers, mushrooms, and onions with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning until fragrant (about **5 min**, stirring occasionally).

### 4. Thicken with flour
Whisk in the flour until combined.

### 5. Build the sauce
Pour in the beef broth and tomato sauce and let it come to a simmer. Then add in the cooked ground turkey.

### 6. Add pasta and finish
Pour in the cooked shells, heavy cream, and Parmesan. Stir to combine.

### 7. Adjust and serve
Taste and adjust salt and pepper or other seasonings as desired. It may seem soupy initially; just simmer and stir occasionally until the sauce thickens. If you'd like it creamier, add more heavy cream. Garnish with fresh basil and more Parmesan.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with a simple side salad or roasted vegetables for a balanced plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with garlic bread to mop up the sauce.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate in an airtight container for **up to 3–4 days**. Reheat in the microwave or on the stovetop, adding a splash of water or extra sauce to keep it from drying out.
- **To freeze:** Cool completely, transfer to an airtight container or freezer-safe bag, label with the date, and freeze for **up to 2–3 months**. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

## Notes

- **Fresh Parmesan matters:** For the best flavor, grate a block yourself or use pre-shredded Parmesan from a bag. Avoid the powdered shaker-can stuff — it's less flavorful and doesn't melt well.
- **Salt in layers:** Season the turkey as it cooks, then adjust again after all ingredients are added.
- **Richer tomato flavor:** Add **1 tbsp** tomato paste with the sautéed veggies.
- **Let it rest:** Let the combined pasta and sauce sit for a few minutes before serving so the pasta absorbs the sauce.
- **Extra seasonings (optional):** Garlic powder, onion powder, fennel seed, red pepper flakes, dried oregano, or a pinch of cayenne.
- **Pasta swaps:** Rotini, penne, or even cheese tortellini work well. If using a full 16-oz box of shells, add more heavy cream or tomato sauce.
- **Pasta bake version:** Cook as written, transfer to an oven-safe dish (or leave in the Dutch oven), top with Parmesan or shredded mozzarella, and bake at **350°F for 20 min** until hot and bubbly.
- **One reader's sub:** Crushed tomatoes pureed in place of tomato sauce, and sour cream instead of heavy cream — reportedly tasted like lasagna.
