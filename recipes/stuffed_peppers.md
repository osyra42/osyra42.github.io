# Stuffed Peppers

**Ready to serve in:** 50 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 35 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **4–6** bell peppers (any color — green, yellow, red, orange)
- [ ] **1 tbsp** olive oil
- [ ] **1 lb** ground beef (or ground turkey, chicken, pork, sausage, or tofu)
- [ ] **1 medium** onion, chopped
- [ ] **1** jalapeño pepper, chopped *(optional, for spicy)*
- [ ] **5 cloves** garlic, chopped
- [ ] **14.5 oz** fire roasted tomatoes (canned — or diced tomatoes or tomato sauce)
- [ ] **1 cup** shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or a blend — pepperjack is great for heat)
- [ ] **1 tbsp** paprika
- [ ] **1 tsp** dried oregano
- [ ] **1 tsp** dried basil
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste
- [ ] **1 cup** cooked rice (white, brown, or wild)
- [ ] Fresh chopped parsley and red pepper flakes, for garnish

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the oven and pot
Preheat your oven to **425°F (220°C)** and bring a large pot of water (enough to cover the bell peppers) to a boil.

### 2. Prep the bell peppers
Wash the bell peppers, cut off the tops, and scoop out the insides (seeds and membranes). You want the inside nice and hollow to accommodate the stuffing. If needed, slice a small part off the bottom so they stand upright. **Don't throw away the tops** — chop them to cook with the onion later (discard the stems).

### 3. Soften the peppers
Blanch the bell peppers for **5 min** to slightly soften. *(Alternative: roast them for 20 min.)* Set the softened peppers into a lightly oiled baking dish.

### 4. Cook the vegetables
Heat the oil in a large pan over **medium heat** and add the onions, chopped pepper tops, and jalapeño (if using). Cook for **5 min** to soften. Add the garlic and cook **1 min**, until fragrant.

### 5. Cook the meat
Add the ground beef and cook **5–6 min**, breaking it apart as you go, until cooked through.

### 6. Add tomatoes, rice, and spices
Add the fire roasted tomatoes, seasonings, and cooked rice. Stir together and remove from heat.

### 7. Add cheese
Stir in **½ cup** of the shredded cheese until incorporated. Add a bit more if you'd like it cheesier.

### 8. Stuff the peppers
Stuff each pepper with the meat filling, then top each with the remaining shredded cheese.

### 9. Roast
Roast the stuffed peppers for **15–20 min**, or until the peppers are tender to your preference and the cheese is bubbly. This is a forgiving recipe — you can usually bake them longer if you want the peppers extra soft.

### 10. Serve
Cool slightly, garnish, and serve.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve on its own — it's a complete meal with protein, veggies, and rice. Skip or reduce the rice for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with garlic bread or a garlic baguette, or a simple side salad.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers in a sealed container for **up to 5 days**. Warm in the oven to enjoy again.
- **Freezer:** Freeze for **up to 2–3 months**.

## Notes

- **Bell pepper sizes vary.** You may need more peppers for the filling, or save any leftover filling for another use — it freezes nicely.
- **Meat swaps:** Ground turkey, chicken, pork, Italian sausage, or a combination all work. Ground turkey or chicken are great lower-calorie options. Use tofu for a vegetarian version.
- **Pepper swaps:** Any color bell works. Other larger peppers like Italian sweet peppers or poblano peppers work too — ideal with thicker-walled peppers that hold up to baking.
- **Cheese options:** Pepperjack is a great spicy option; cheddar, mozzarella, or a blend all work. Try a combination.
- **Seasoning ideas:** Adjust to your preference — Cajun seasonings or Italian seasoning blends work well. Worcestershire sauce is a popular addition.
- **Make it a casserole:** If you'd rather skip the individual peppers, the site has a separate stuffed pepper casserole recipe.
- **No-oven option (reader tip):** Steam the peppers instead of boiling or roasting (uses just an inch of water), and bake in an air fryer to avoid heating up the kitchen.
- **Reader additions that work:** Mushrooms, Worcestershire sauce, smoked paprika, and a cheddar + pepperjack cheese blend.
