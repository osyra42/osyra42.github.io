# Chicken Quesadillas

**Ready to serve in:** 30 min

**Prep:** 20 min | **Cook:** 10 min | **Yields:** 8 quesadillas

## Ingredients

### Seasoning Mix

- [ ] **1 tsp** salt (coarse, kosher)
- [ ] **½ tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** dried oregano
- [ ] **½ tsp** cumin
- [ ] **½ tsp** chili powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** paprika (smoked or regular)
- [ ] **¼ tsp** black pepper (coarsely ground)

### Quesadillas

- [ ] **4 cups** cooked, chopped chicken
- [ ] **1 tbsp** oil
- [ ] **2–3 cups** sliced mushrooms *(optional)*
- [ ] **2 medium** bell peppers (any color), cored and sliced into thin strips
- [ ] **1 large** onion, sliced into thin half-moon strips
- [ ] **2–3 tbsp** broth or water
- [ ] **2 cups** shredded Monterey Jack cheese
- [ ] **1 cup** shredded medium or sharp cheddar cheese
- [ ] **8** 8-inch flour tortillas

## Instructions

### 1. Mix the seasoning
In a small bowl, add all the seasoning ingredients. Mix well.

### 2. Season the chicken
Toss the chicken with **half** of the seasoning mixture. Set aside.

### 3. Cook the vegetables
In a 12-inch nonstick skillet, heat the oil over **medium heat**. Add the mushrooms (if using), onions, and bell peppers. Sprinkle the remaining seasoning mixture over the veggies. Cook, stirring often, until the onions start to turn translucent and the vegetables start to soften.

### 4. Add liquid
Add **2–3 tbsp** water or broth (omit this if there's already liquid in the pan from the mushrooms or other veggies). Continue cooking over medium heat for **2–3 min** until the veggies are crisp-tender and the liquid has reduced slightly.

### 5. Assemble
Spread cheese on **half** of each tortilla. Top the cheese with chicken, veggies, more cheese, and then fold the tortilla in half over the filling.

### 6. Cook the quesadillas
Heat a 12-inch nonstick skillet (or griddle) over **medium or medium-low heat**. Add a touch of oil to the pan for a crispier quesadilla. Cook the quesadillas on one side until golden, moderating the heat as needed, pressing lightly with a spatula to compress and flatten a bit if desired. Carefully flip with the spatula and cook on the other side until the tortilla is golden and the filling and cheese are hot and melted.

### 7. Serve
Cut into triangles and serve immediately. (Or keep the cooked quesadillas warm on a baking sheet in a **200°F** oven until ready to serve.)

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with sliced avocado and chopped cilantro, or skip the tortilla and pile the filling over greens for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with sour cream, salsa, avocados, and chopped cilantro for topping. Mexican street corn salad, cornbread, or fresh fruit all work well on the side.

## Storage Tips

- **Refrigerate:** Cooked quesadillas keep in the fridge for **3–4 days**. They're best reheated in a skillet or air fryer for crispy shells.
- **Freeze:** Well-covered, they keep in the freezer for **several months**. Reheat covered in foil in a **300°F** oven for **15–20 min**, or in the microwave, skillet, or air fryer.
- **Make ahead:** Assemble ahead and store in the fridge. Reheat as above.

## Notes

- **Pro tip — add liquid:** Adding **2–3 tbsp** water or broth near the end of cooking the veggies creates a simple "sauce" as the liquid absorbs the flavor of the seasoning and veggies. Most importantly, it keeps the quesadillas from being dry.
- **Veggie variations:** Any color or variety of bell pepper works. Add jalapeños for heat, use red or yellow onions, add extra mushrooms or leave them out, or throw in corn kernels, zucchini, or any other vegetable you like.
- **Cheese:** Freshly grated Monterey Jack and cheddar melt better than pre-shredded. A pre-shredded Mexican blend works too — Monterey Jack melts better and creamier than mozzarella.
- **Meat swaps:** Leave the meat out and amp up the veggies for a veggie quesadilla. Or swap in ground beef or ground turkey — if doing so, add the seasoning mixture while the meat cooks.
- **Tortilla size:** The recipe uses 8-inch tortillas, but smaller or larger ones work depending on what you have.
- **Cooking tips:** Use the same pan the veggies cooked in for the quesadillas (easy cleanup). Wipe it clean with a paper towel first, then add a small splash of oil before cooking.
- **Assembly tip (reader note):** Move quickly during assembly, or the filling will cool off before the quesadillas hit the pan.
