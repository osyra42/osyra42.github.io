# Baked Fish

**Ready to serve in:** 25 min

**Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **½ tsp** seasoned salt
- [ ] **½ tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **1 tsp** lemon zest
- [ ] **¼ tsp** black pepper, to taste
- [ ] **1½ lbs** fish fillets (halibut, cod, or tilapia)
- [ ] **3 tbsp** butter, melted
- [ ] **¼ cup** Parmesan cheese, shredded
- [ ] Fresh parsley, for garnish *(optional)*
- [ ] Lemon wedges, for serving *(optional)*

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the seasonings and dish
Combine the seasoned salt, garlic powder, onion powder, lemon zest, and black pepper in a small bowl. Spray a baking dish with cooking spray, or grease with olive oil.

### 2. Season the fish
Place the fish fillets in the greased baking dish and sprinkle with the seasoning mix. Grate lemon zest over the top of the fish.

### 3. Add butter and Parmesan
Drizzle the melted butter evenly over the fillets, then grate the Parmesan over the top.

### 4. Bake
Cover with foil and bake at **350°F** for **15–20 min**, or until the fish flakes easily with a fork. Check at **15 min** — cook time will depend on the thickness of your fish.

### 5. Serve
Serve with extra lemon butter from the baking pan. Sprinkle with parsley and extra Parmesan if desired.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with steamed green beans or a simple side salad for a lighter, lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with honey mustard roasted potatoes, mashed potatoes, or another starch to round out the meal.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for **up to 3 days**.
- Reheat gently in the oven or microwave until warmed through — fish dries out quickly, so keep an eye on it.
- Best enjoyed fresh; freezing is not recommended.

## Notes

- **Fish choice:** Halibut and cod are ideal because they're nice and thick. Tilapia works too but cooks faster (as little as **15 min** total) since it's thinner.
- **Doneness rule of thumb:** Allow **6–8 min per 8 oz** of fish, or **10 min per inch of thickness**. The fish is done when it flakes with a fork and is opaque, not translucent.
- **Err on the side of less:** Underdone fish can go back in the oven; overdone fish is ruined. Check early.
- **No baking dish?** Use a rimmed baking sheet instead.
- **Lemons:** Toss used lemon halves into the baking dish while the fish bakes, or slice and place on top.
- **Alternative method:** Combine the melted butter, lemon zest, and seasonings, then pour over the fish instead of sprinkling. A splash of white wine in the mix is a nice addition.
- **Simpler version:** Skip the garlic and onion powder and season with just salt and pepper.
