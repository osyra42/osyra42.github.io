# Chicken Chili

**Ready to serve in:** 2 hrs 15 min (or 6–8 hrs on LOW)

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 2 hrs | **Yields:** 6–8 servings (about 10 cups)

## Ingredients

- [ ] **2–2½ lbs** boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- [ ] **1 large** yellow onion, diced
- [ ] **2 stalks** celery, diced
- [ ] **2 (15-oz) cans** tomato puree (about **3 cups**)
- [ ] **1 cup** chicken broth or stock
- [ ] **4 cloves** garlic, minced or pressed
- [ ] **2 tbsp** chili powder
- [ ] **1 tsp** cumin
- [ ] **1 tsp** oregano
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt
- [ ] **2 (15-oz) cans** black beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] **1 cup** frozen corn kernels
- [ ] **1 tbsp** cider vinegar

### Optional Chili Toppings

- [ ] Diced green onions
- [ ] Shredded cheese
- [ ] Diced avocado
- [ ] Chopped cilantro
- [ ] Sour cream

## Instructions

### 1. Combine vegetables and chicken
If using chicken breasts, cut them into 2 or 3 large pieces. Nestle the chicken into the vegetables inside the slow cooker.

### 2. Add liquids and spices
Add the tomato puree, broth, garlic, and spices to the slow cooker. Stir to mix and coat the chicken and vegetables. **Wait to add the beans, corn, and vinegar until the end of cooking.**

### 3. Cook
Cover and cook on **HIGH for 2–4 hrs**, or **LOW for 6–8 hrs**. It's fine if the chili cooks a little longer on LOW or WARM — the chicken just gets more tender the longer it cooks. Try not to push it too much longer, or the chicken starts to become mushy.

### 4. Shred the chicken
Shred the chicken right in the slow cooker using two forks, or transfer the large pieces to a bowl, shred, and return to the slow cooker. Taste and add salt, pepper, or additional spices if desired.

### 5. Add beans, corn, and vinegar
Stir them in, then cover and continue cooking until warmed through — about **15–30 min**. Watch for the chili to start bubbling again.

### 6. Serve
Ladle into bowls and top with your choice of toppings.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve as-is for a hearty, high-protein bowl, or over cauliflower rice. Top with avocado, cilantro, and a dollop of sour cream.
- **For the family:** Serve with crushed tortilla chips, shredded cheese, and cornbread on the side.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers for **up to 5 days**.
- **Freeze:** Airtight containers for **up to 3 months**. Thaw in the fridge before reheating, or warm frozen chili right in a covered saucepan over low heat.

## Nutrition

Per serving (from the source):
- **406** calories
- **12g** fat
- **35g** carbs
- **44g** protein

## Notes

- **Chicken thighs are best.** They hold up better than breasts over the long cooking time and shred into silky, tender strands. Chicken breasts work but the texture will be slightly chewier. You can also use a mix.
- **Flexible timing:** This chili is very forgiving. If you're running late, it's fine if it continues on LOW or WARM past the 8-hour mark — just don't push it too far or the chicken gets mushy.
- **Less tomato-y:** Reduce the tomato puree to one can and add extra broth to compensate.
- **More spice:** Add **1–2** chopped jalapeños or a **4-oz** can of roasted green chiles at the beginning of cooking.
- **Bean swap:** Pinto beans work in place of black beans.
- **Topping ideas:** Cilantro, green onions, avocado, crushed tortilla chips, sour cream, or extra cheese.
- **Reader swaps that work:** Omit the celery or onion (use onion powder instead), swap in dark kidney beans, add extra corn (with more broth to compensate), or replace the onion, celery, tomato, and spices with two jars of salsa for a cheat version.
