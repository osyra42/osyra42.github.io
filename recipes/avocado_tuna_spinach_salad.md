# Avocado Tuna Spinach Salad

**Ready to serve in:** 10 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 0 min | **Yields:** 1 serving

## Ingredients

- [ ] **½ (5-oz) can** water-packed tuna
- [ ] **¼ cup** diced avocado
- [ ] **¼ cup** halved cherry tomatoes
- [ ] **1½ tbsp** poppy seed dressing
- [ ] **1 tbsp** diced red onion
- [ ] **1 tbsp** extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] **2 cups** baby spinach
- [ ] **1 tbsp** sunflower seeds

## Instructions

### 1. Combine
Combine tuna, avocado, tomatoes, dressing, onion, and oil in a medium bowl.

### 2. Serve
Serve over spinach and sprinkle with sunflower seeds.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Enjoy as-is for a light, high-protein lunch or a quick no-cook dinner.
- **For the family:** Double or triple the recipe for a shared lunch. Pairs well with crusty bread or a bowl of soup on the side.

## Storage Tips

- Best eaten fresh — the avocado will brown and the spinach will wilt if stored.
- If making ahead, combine the tuna mixture and store separately from the spinach, then assemble just before serving.

## Nutrition

## Notes

- **No-cook meal:** Everything comes together in one bowl — great for hot days or a quick lunch.
- **Tuna swap:** One reader used a can of yellowfin tuna packed in olive oil and skipped the dressing entirely — still delicious.
- **Dressing swap:** French dressing in place of the poppy seed dressing and olive oil works well and needs less.
- **Nutrition profile:** Nut-free, soy-free, egg-free, gluten-free, high-fiber, high-protein, low-calorie.
