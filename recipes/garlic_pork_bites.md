# Garlic Pork Bites

**Ready to serve in:** 45 min

**Prep:** 20 min | **Cook:** 25 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

### For the Pork

- [ ] **4** boneless pork chops (approximately **2 lbs**), cut into 1-inch pieces
- [ ] **1 tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **1 tsp** kosher salt
- [ ] **½ tsp** black pepper
- [ ] **1 tbsp** extra-virgin olive oil

### For the Sauce

- [ ] **¼ cup (½ stick)** unsalted butter
- [ ] **1 tsp** garlic, minced
- [ ] **1 tbsp** all-purpose flour
- [ ] **½ cup** chicken broth
- [ ] **½ cup** heavy cream
- [ ] **½ cup** grated Parmesan cheese
- [ ] **2 tbsp** lemon juice
- [ ] Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

### 1. Season the pork
Season pork evenly with onion powder, salt, and pepper.

### 2. Sear the pork
In a large 12-inch skillet, heat olive oil over **medium heat**. When the oil is hot, add the pork and sear for **3–5 min on all sides** until cooked through (about **13–15 min total**). Pork is cooked through when it reaches an internal temperature of **145°F**.

### 3. Set aside
Remove the pork from the skillet and set it on a plate. Tent with foil to keep it warm.

### 4. Start the sauce
In the same skillet, melt the butter. Add the garlic and cook for **1 min**. Whisk in the flour until slightly thickened.

### 5. Build the sauce
Add the chicken broth, heavy cream, Parmesan cheese, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

### 6. Simmer
Let the sauce simmer for **5–8 min**, or until thickened.

### 7. Finish
Add the pork bites back to the pan and toss them in the sauce to coat. Serve immediately.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve over mashed potatoes or with a side of green beans or asparagus for a balanced plate.
- **For the family:** Serve over mashed potatoes, pasta, or rice — the creamy garlic sauce is great for sopping up. Honey roasted carrots work well on the side too.

## Storage Tips

- Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for **up to 3–4 days**.
- **Reheat** in a skillet over **medium heat** or in the microwave until heated through. The garlic butter sauce may thicken slightly when chilled, but it loosens up again as it warms.

## Notes

- **Pork cut:** Boneless pork chops work best. Choose the **thicker cut** (about **1½ inches** thick) — they're not as easy to overcook as thin-cut chops. Boneless pork chops are lean with a mild flavor, but they'll pick up plenty of flavor from the garlic sauce.
- **Doneness:** Pork is safe to eat at **145°F**. Use an instant-read thermometer to check.
- **Sauce color:** One reader asked why the sauce wasn't brown like the photo. It's actually meant to be creamy/Alfredo-like — the source doesn't brown the flour, so the sauce stays light.
- **If using whole milk instead of heavy cream:** Replace 1:1, simmer **2–4 min longer** to reduce slightly, and optionally whisk **1 tsp** cornstarch into the milk first for a texture closer to the original. Or use **½ cup** whole milk + **1 tbsp** butter for a richer result. The sauce will be thinner and less velvety.
- **Slow cooker note:** This recipe isn't ideal for the slow cooker — the quick, high heat of the skillet gives the pork good browning and keeps it juicy. If you do use a slow cooker, cook on **LOW for 3–4 hrs** with a little broth or sauce so it doesn't dry out, but expect a different texture.
- **Reader swaps and additions that work well:**
  - Shiitake or cremini mushrooms (sauté them first)
  - Dijon or grainy mustard
  - Cornstarch instead of flour
  - Omit the Parmesan if you're not a fan
  - Pork belly or smoked pork shoulder cubes instead of chops (both work)
  - Increase the broth and cream slightly for extra gravy
