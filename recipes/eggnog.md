# Eggnog

**Ready to serve in:** 25 min + chill time

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 10 min | **Yields:** 6 servings (about 4 cups)

## Ingredients

- [ ] **6 large** egg yolks
- [ ] **½ cup** granulated sugar
- [ ] **1 cup** heavy whipping cream
- [ ] **2 cups** milk
- [ ] **½ tsp** ground nutmeg
- [ ] Pinch of salt
- [ ] **¼ tsp** vanilla extract
- [ ] Ground cinnamon, for topping
- [ ] Alcohol, *optional* (see Notes)

## Instructions

### 1. Whisk the eggs and sugar
Whisk the egg yolks and sugar together in a medium bowl until light and creamy.

### 2. Heat the dairy
In a saucepan over **medium-high heat**, combine the cream, milk, nutmeg, and salt. Stir often until the mixture reaches a bare simmer.

### 3. Temper the eggs
Add a big spoonful of the hot milk to the egg mixture, whisking vigorously. Repeat, adding a big spoonful at a time, to temper the eggs.

### 4. Combine and cook
Once most of the hot milk has been added to the eggs, pour the mixture back into the saucepan. Whisk constantly for **1 min or so**, until just slightly thickened (or until it reaches about **160°F** on a thermometer). It will thicken more as it cools.

### 5. Finish
Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla and alcohol, if using.

### 6. Strain and chill
Pour the eggnog through a fine mesh strainer into a pitcher or other container, and cover with plastic wrap. Refrigerate until chilled. It will thicken as it cools. For a thinner, completely smooth consistency, add the entire mixture to a blender with **1–2 tbsp** milk and blend until smooth.

### 7. Serve
Serve with a sprinkle of cinnamon or nutmeg, and fresh whipped cream if desired.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve in a mug with a dusting of cinnamon or nutmeg and a cinnamon stick.
- **For the family:** Top with whipped cream and a pinch of nutmeg. Also makes a great **eggnog latte** — warm **½–¾ cup** eggnog and add **1–2 shots** espresso (or **½ cup** strong coffee). Top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of nutmeg or cinnamon.

## Storage Tips

- Store homemade eggnog in the fridge for **up to 1 week**.
- Adding alcohol does **not** significantly extend the shelf life — still best within one week.

## Nutrition

## Notes

- **Yield:** About **4 cups**.
- **Alcohol (optional):** If adding alcohol, start with **¼ cup** brandy, bourbon, rum, or whisky, added at the same time as the vanilla or after cooling. Add more to taste. High-alcohol spirits work best to counterbalance the sweetness.
- **Dairy swaps:** Light cream works in place of heavy cream, but the eggnog will be a little less rich and thick. Coconut cream + coconut milk should work since this is an egg-based custard, though the source hasn't tested it — expect a mild coconut flavor and slightly less rich texture.
- **Storage note:** The source recommends a week max in the fridge, even with alcohol added.
- **Related idea:** This same custard base is essentially an ice cream base — one reader noted it tastes like unfrozen nutmeg ice cream.
