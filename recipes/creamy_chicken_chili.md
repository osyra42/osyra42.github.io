# Creamy Chicken Chili

**Ready to serve in:** 6–7 hrs 15 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 6–7 hrs LOW or 3–4 hrs HIGH | **Yields:** ~6 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1.5 lbs** boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or thighs for juicier)
- [ ] **1** medium onion, diced (use half for lower carbs)
- [ ] **1** bell pepper (any color), diced
- [ ] **3–4 cloves** garlic, minced (or **1 tsp** garlic powder)
- [ ] **1 can** (4 oz) diced green chiles
- [ ] **1.5 cups** low-sodium chicken broth
- [ ] **1 tbsp** chili powder
- [ ] **2 tsp** ground cumin
- [ ] **1 tsp** dried oregano
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste
- [ ] **4 oz** cream cheese, softened and cubed (this is the key!)
- [ ] **½ cup** heavy cream
- [ ] **Juice of 1** lime
- [ ] Fresh cilantro, for garnish

### Optional Toppings

- [ ] Sliced avocado
- [ ] Shredded cheese
- [ ] Sour cream or Greek yogurt

## Instructions

### 1. Layer the base
Place chicken, onion, bell pepper, garlic, and green chiles in the crockpot.

### 2. Add seasonings & liquid
Sprinkle chili powder, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper over everything. Pour in chicken broth (shouldn't completely cover the chicken).

### 3. Cook
Cover and cook on **LOW for 6–7 hrs** or **HIGH for 3–4 hrs**, until chicken is tender and shreds easily.

### 4. Shred
Remove chicken to a cutting board, shred with two forks, then return to crockpot.

### 5. Make it creamy
Turn crockpot to **HIGH**. Add softened cream cheese and heavy cream. Stir until cream cheese is fully melted and smooth (about **5 min**).

### 6. Finish
Stir in lime juice. Taste and adjust salt/pepper. Top with cilantro and avocado.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve as-is for a hearty, low-carb chili bowl, or over cauliflower rice.
- **For the family:** Serve over rice or with tortilla chips and toppings.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate in an airtight container for **up to 4 days**.
- Freezes well for **up to 3 months** – thaw overnight and reheat gently.

## Nutrition

- **Low-carb** – cream cheese and heavy cream keep it rich without starches.
- **High-protein** – chicken provides a lean, satisfying base.
- **Customizable** – adjust spice level with extra chili powder or jalapeños!
