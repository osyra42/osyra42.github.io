# Ground Turkey Taco Meat

**Ready to serve in:** 30 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Yields:** 3 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1½ tbsp** chili powder
- [ ] **2 tsp** ground cumin
- [ ] **1 tsp** ground paprika
- [ ] **1 tsp** salt
- [ ] **½ tsp** garlic powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** onion powder
- [ ] **½ tsp** dried oregano
- [ ] **¼ tsp** cayenne pepper
- [ ] **12 oz** ground turkey
- [ ] **½ cup** water
- [ ] **1 tbsp** cider vinegar
- [ ] **1½ tsp** brown sugar

## Instructions

### 1. Mix the spice blend
Combine chili powder, cumin, paprika, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, and cayenne in a small bowl.

### 2. Brown the turkey
Heat a large nonstick skillet over **medium heat**. Add ground turkey and cook, stirring to break up clumps, until no longer pink, about **5 min**.

### 3. Add the spices
Stir in the spice mixture and water. Reduce heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, until most of the liquid is absorbed, about **10 min**.

### 4. Finish
Stir in cider vinegar and brown sugar; simmer until flavors combine, **3–4 min**.

### 5. Serve
Transfer to a serving bowl. Serve and enjoy!

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve in lettuce wraps or over a taco salad with greens, avocado, and cheese for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve in warm flour or corn tortillas with cilantro, chopped onion, and lime wedges. Also great as a topping for baked potatoes.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for **up to 4 days**.
- Reheat gently in a skillet or microwave until heated through.
- The spice blend can be mixed up in batches ahead of time and stored in the pantry for quick dinners.

## Nutrition

Per serving (from the source):
- **201** calories
- **10g** fat
- **6g** carbs
- **21g** protein

## Notes

- A little spicy by design — cut back on the cayenne if you prefer it milder.
- This is a very adaptable recipe. Home cooks frequently swap in real garlic and onion for the powders, use chicken stock or broth instead of water, or add a can of rinsed beans or diced tomatoes with green chiles.
- A squeeze of lime juice or a splash of vinegar brightens it up nicely.
- Makes an excellent base for chili or a taco salad.
