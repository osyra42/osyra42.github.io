# Spinach Mushroom Pork Chops

**Ready to serve in:** 30 min

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Yields:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **4** pork chops (boneless or bone-in, 0.75–1" thick)
- [ ] Salt & pepper to taste
- [ ] **1 tbsp** olive oil
- [ ] **2 tbsp** butter, divided
- [ ] **8 oz** cremini or white mushrooms, sliced
- [ ] **3 cloves** garlic, minced
- [ ] **¼ tsp** Italian seasoning
- [ ] **1 tbsp** flour
- [ ] **½ cup** chicken broth
- [ ] **½ tsp** lemon juice
- [ ] **¾ cup** heavy/whipping cream
- [ ] **2 cups** (packed) fresh baby spinach

## Instructions

### 1. Season the pork
Take the pork chops out of the fridge **15–30 min** before starting if possible. Season generously with salt & pepper on both sides.

### 2. Sear the pork
In a deep skillet, add the olive oil and **1 tbsp** of the butter over **medium-high heat**. Let the pan heat up for a few minutes, then cook the pork chops for **3–5 min per side** or until golden (3 min for thinner chops, 5 min for chops up to 1" thick). Transfer to a plate.

### 3. Sauté the mushrooms
Add the remaining butter, mushrooms, garlic, and Italian seasoning to the skillet. Cook for about **5–6 min** or until the mushrooms have released their water and it's just about cooked off.

### 4. Add the flour
Sprinkle the flour in and cook for about **1 min**, stirring nearly constantly.

### 5. Build the sauce
Add the chicken broth and lemon juice. Stir well so the flour dissolves, and let it cook for **1–2 min** or until the liquid is reduced by half.

### 6. Add the cream
Stir in the cream.

### 7. Finish the pork
Add the pork back into the skillet and cook for another **3–5 min** or until the sauce has thickened a bit (let it bubble gently, not furiously boil) and the pork is fully cooked through (**145°F** minimum). Keep in mind the pork's temperature will continue to rise once you stop cooking it.

### 8. Wilt the spinach
Add in the spinach (lift the pork chops with tongs and tuck it under them). Take the pan off the heat and let the spinach wilt for **1–2 min**. Season the sauce with extra salt & pepper if needed, then serve immediately.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Serve with steamed veggies, sweet corn, or a side salad for a lower-carb plate.
- **For the family:** Serve with mashed potatoes, pasta, Stick of Butter Rice, or crusty bread to mop up the cream sauce.

## Storage Tips

- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for **3–4 days**.
- Reheat in the oven or in a saucepan at a low temperature until heated through.
- Freezing is **not recommended** for pork with a cream sauce.

## Nutrition

- **Low-carb friendly** – the carbs come mainly from the mushrooms and spinach.
- **High-protein** – pork chops provide a lean, satisfying base.
- **Rich & creamy** – heavy cream makes the sauce luscious; lower-fat substitutes will thin it out and may curdle.

## Notes

- You can use boneless or bone-in pork chops. **0.75–1" thick** is ideal — anything thicker or thinner is likely to end up under or overcooked since the chops finish in the cream sauce.
- It's very easy to overcook pork. An instant-read thermometer takes the guesswork out — **145°F** and slightly pink inside is safe to eat.
- Fresh spinach works best. If using frozen, thaw it first and squeeze the water out really well.
- Feel free to change up the mushroom variety.
