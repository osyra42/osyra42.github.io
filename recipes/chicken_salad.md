# Chicken Salad

**Ready to serve in:** 15 min

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 0 min | **Yields:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- [ ] **2 cups** cooked chopped or shredded chicken
- [ ] **½ cup** mayonnaise
- [ ] **1 rib** celery, diced
- [ ] **1** green onion, thinly sliced
- [ ] **1 tsp** Dijon mustard
- [ ] **¼ tsp** seasoned salt, more to taste
- [ ] **⅛ tsp** black pepper, or to taste
- [ ] **1 tsp** chopped fresh dill (or **¼ tsp** dried), *optional*

## Instructions

### 1. Combine
In a medium bowl, add the chicken, mayonnaise, celery, green onion, mustard, salt, pepper, and dill if using. Mix well to combine.

### 2. Taste and adjust
Taste and season with additional salt and pepper if desired.

### 3. Serve
Serve as a sandwich or over salad.

## Serving Suggestions

- **For you:** Spoon over greens or into lettuce leaves for lettuce wraps, or serve with crackers for a lighter option.
- **For the family:** Pile onto rolls, bread, or a croissant — lightly toasted is best. Great for a quick lunch or light dinner.

## Storage Tips

- Refrigerate in an airtight container for **up to 5 days**. Stir before serving.
- **Freezer:** Prepared chicken salad doesn't freeze well. To prep ahead, dice and freeze the cooked chicken, then combine with the remaining ingredients once thawed.

## Nutrition

Per serving (from the source):
- **206** calories
- **16g** fat (3g saturated)
- **1g** carbs (1g fiber, 1g sugar)
- **15g** protein
- **48mg** cholesterol | **362mg** sodium

## Notes

- **Chicken options:** Rotisserie chicken is the easiest since it's already cooked. Leftover grilled chicken, baked chicken breasts or thighs, or even canned chicken all work. If using canned, mix everything except the chicken first, then gently fold in the drained chicken.
- **Onion swap:** Replace the green onion with chives or finely diced red onion if desired.
- **Optional additions:**
  - **1 cup** chopped red grapes or **1 cup** chopped peeled apple
  - **2 tbsp** chopped nuts (pecans or almonds)
- **Variations:**
  - Add dill or other fresh herbs.
  - Lighten it up by replacing half the mayo with Greek yogurt.
  - Change the flavor by adding ranch dressing.
  - Dress it up with chopped apples, grapes, or nuts.
