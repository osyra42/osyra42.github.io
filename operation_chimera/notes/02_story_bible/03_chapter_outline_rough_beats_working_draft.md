#### Overall shape (author, 2026-07-24)

Four parts, character-first (the case/hunts are the frame; the book is really her, the other Chimerans, and reclaiming a self). ~15-30 chapters per part - this is a long book.

- **Part 1** - origin through **boot-camp graduation** (the making of the operator). Ends at age ~20 in the forest (where *Borne Weapon* ends).
- **Part 2** - **the hunts**, working effectively *with* the Agency. Episodic - multiple entity/Chimera hunts. Ages ~20-29.
- **Part 3** - **after she leaves** the Agency, through **winning the legal case**. Posting -> no traction -> Titan Reach's reclamation suit -> trial -> win. See legal_premise.md.
- **Part 4** - **independent contractor**, hunting entities on her own terms.

Anchored to timeline.md / history.md (ages are the spine, dates are not fixed). Existing written stories are threaded in, not replaced.
