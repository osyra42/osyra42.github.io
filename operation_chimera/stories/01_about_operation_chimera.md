## About Operation Chimera

Osyra Renaya is my original character, and the face I use online - the VTuber model I show up as instead of a camera. Operation Chimera is where her story actually gets written down.

She was sixteen when a lab took her apart and put her back together as something part wolf, part human. She lived. Most of the other kids didn't. Now she works for the Agency that cleans up after things like the ones that made her - a Chimeran Operator, callsign Chimera One, sent after the anomalies nobody else comes back from.

The Agency is not the good guys, the company that funded the lab is still in business, and Osyra spends most of her life deciding how much of herself she's willing to spend on a job that was never going to love her back. She saves people because it's the work. That's usually the only reason on offer.

Each book stands on its own and covers a different stretch of her life.
