# Borne Weapon - Rev4 Outline

Talking-point reference. Per chapter, not per scene.

## I. Chapter 1 - The Application

- A. Records a video application in a dark room, hood up, glasses glared out
- B. Recites her history flat: given up at six, foster care, sold to a facility at sixteen
- C. Names the lab: illegal hybridization experiments, most subjects died, she didn't
- D. Pulls the hood back herself - the reveal is theater, they already knew; the point is owning who does it
- E. Vasquez replies within five minutes: a form letter, no reaction, verification required at 09:00
- F. She'd braced for revulsion and got a scheduling requirement - and prefers it
- G. Types "Confirmed"

## II. Chapter 2 - The Verification

- A. Bad night, sensory replay of the lab; the delight in a scientist's voice is what stays
- B. Vasquez keeps her waiting a full minute on purpose
- C. Ordered through absurd tasks: shoulder rolls, twice, slower
- D. Told to pinch her own ear and hold it out - made to examine herself on command
- E. Eye tracking, then bare the canines. "Satisfactory" as a stamp, not praise
- F. In-person clinical scheduled for 14:00 same day - five hours, not enough time to back out
- G. Verbal confirmation demanded; a nod isn't a record
- H. Realizes the rotations had no clinical value - the test was obedience, and she passed

## III. Chapter 3 - The Clinical

- A. Lobby processing: cup, forms, gown with a purpose-built tail hole
- B. Waiting room recruits trade rumors about what the tests are secretly for
- C. Assembly line: blood draws, body scan, coordination, standing jump
- D. Technician trained not to react - visibly working at it, which is worse than staring
- E. Musculoskeletal exam under Vasquez and Chen; chases a laser dot for ten minutes
- F. Recognizes the cadence of the notes - same sentences as her lab file, same profession
- G. Chen clicks a clicker behind her; her ears answer him. The only part she'd take back
- H. Chen calls her tail "a fully functional mechanism" - to Vasquez, not to her
- I. Dr. Ellen and the reproductive baseline: ultrasound, then the question nobody has ever asked
- J. Describes the annual fall cycle out loud for the first time in her life
- K. Diagnosed as photoperiod-triggered canid estrus; hormonal regulators mandated, couriered
- L. Can't find the objection - the help and the leash are the same object
- M. "You have passed." Something in her sits up at being graded, and that's the worst outcome

## IV. Chapter 4 - The Rooftop

- A. Pill kit arrives; she leaves it on the counter untouched. A tiny rebellion she knows is tiny
- B. Drive window hits on schedule; apartment becomes unbearable; she goes to the roof
- C. Catches Leo's scent and crosses the roof before deciding to - the thing she fears most
- D. He doesn't run. Reads her ears as symptoms, not costume. Asks what she needs
- E. She touches his hand; he turns it palm up; the frenzy channels into an anchor
- F. Not passion - a quieting. First time she isn't a subject or a monster
- G. Goes inside, feels the touch turn into a smudge, takes one pill as an eraser
- H. Morning "clarity": Leo reclassified from person to variable
- I. Retrospective: the pill didn't add information, it changed the verdict - toward what the company wanted
- J. Never takes a second one. One was enough; she does the rest of the erasing herself for free

## V. Chapter 5 - The Offer

- A. Lt. Col. Daniel Thorne calls - military voice, Titan Reach liaison
- B. Offer: a combine, a final evaluation, guaranteed job if she passes
- C. "Failure has permanent consequences" - the gap left deliberately for her to fill in
- D. 48 hours to decide, NDA to sign, or the conversation never happened
- E. Her arithmetic: can't trust her biology, can't trust her judgment, so outsource the trust
- F. Retrospective: every piece of evidence in that proof was manufactured by the people making the offer
- G. The one clean data point - Leo - she'd already thrown out and called it clarity
- H. Signs. Shame is a sharper spur than money. Coordinates, 04:30, pack light
- I. Leaves the pills on the counter

## VI. Chapter 6 - The Combine

- A. Silent SUV, unmarked plane, two other recruits, a bunker in evergreen forest
- B. Wristband, retina scan, follow the green line
- C. Gymnasium intake: the stares, the murmurs, "Fido," "wrong door, Furry"
- D. Distinguishes the shame-heat from the biological heat - one has a dosage, one doesn't
- E. Pulled out for custom fitting; armorer calls her "the chimera candidate" while she stands there
- F. Notices the rhythm: every time the room adjusts to her, they remove her and she comes back new
- G. Medical bay: tailored vaccines for "the non-human cellular lines," said to the file
- H. Understands the mechanism - fifty terrified people need somewhere to put fear, and it goes sideways to the furthest thing from the middle
- I. Stumps escalates to the rabies joke

## VII. Chapter 7 - The Prototype

- A. A lean grey-eyed recruit shuts Evers/Stumps down - not on her behalf
- B. Reframes her publicly as a deep-bio prototype: spine, ears, eyes all engineered for function
- C. She can hear him inventing it - and he's accidentally right, off a wrong proof
- D. "They built a super-soldier. And you're calling it names." Room goes silent
- E. He gives her a nod of fanatic approval and walks off
- F. She corrects herself: he didn't defend her, he moved her from one shelf to another
- G. But fear is a cleaner barrier than contempt - it gives room
- H. The trade, laid out honestly: pet or weapon, and weapon is cheaper
- I. Takes the role. Notices she's volunteering to be what the lab built, and nothing moves

## VIII. Chapter 8 - Shark Attack

- A. Mockery dies; she perfects the flat stare and finds the lever marked *be worse* works instantly
- B. Custom uniform issued: same garment, special version - standardized and apart at once
- C. Helmet comes in its own hard case; everyone else's goes in a duffel
- D. Pre-dawn formation: Sergeant names her Renaya - the first use of her name, to explain she isn't one of them
- E. The red-taped seat at the back of the bus. Someone filed a work order for that tape
- F. The Shark Attack: thirty minutes of screaming at fifty people
- G. He never singles her out. "You are nothing," aimed at everyone equally
- H. Best she has felt in two years - the entire price of admission to the human race is finding someone who hates everyone equally
- I. Off the bus, re-isolated within thirty minutes: ten feet left of the formation, "you are a shadow"
- J. Ten feet is not far. You could hand somebody a cup of coffee across it

## IX. Chapter 9 - Broken Down

- A. First week is democratic humiliation - collective punishment, dumped footlockers, push-ups on gravel
- B. Admires the machine: you build loyalty by charging everyone for one person's mistake
- C. It can't work on her - you can't be a cell of an organism from ten feet outside it
- D. Height as permanent landscape feature; DI beats the slumping out of her
- E. Spends the month running slower than she can run so a line of men can stay in front
- F. Respiratory plague sweeps the bay; her engineered immunity keeps her healthy, which isolates her further
- G. Armory: issued the Mk 13, thirteen pounds, the first object ever handed to her that was the right size
- H. DIs never mock her - they punish everyone else for looking. Discipline built out of the material she is
- I. Briggs hand-off drill: he sinks under the rifle, she takes him down before she decides to
- J. "Controlled" is the only part she contributed - the machinery let her have the throttle
- K. "The arm is built to carry it." She becomes a distraction hazard, which is the most anonymity she's had
- L. Chow hall: fifteen minutes a day, the only place she gets to use the word *we*

## X. Chapter 10 - The Trials

- A. Gas chamber: mask off, name and rank, the great equalizer
- B. Exactly as bad for her as for everyone. The bay's respiratory plague is violently cured
- C. Took a shed full of poison gas to get her admitted to the group
- D. Night ops: NVGs are worse than her own eyes; she stows them
- E. Vance permits it; she quietly steers her squad and they're the only one without injuries
- F. Looks shift from fear to grim practical acknowledgment - another trade, and she's counting them now
- G. PT tests: she isn't smug, she's impatient - starts shouting encouragement at Miller and Carter
- H. Checks whether it was kindness, decides it was pragmatism - cells in an organism
- I. Retrospective: she was never in the organism, so the proof was worthless. She just wanted him to make it
- J. Becomes a leader by default - calls alignment, barks the strap drill
- K. The balm: authority that locates the problem somewhere other than her body. Twelve years waiting for it
- L. The lab's appetite pointed at a mission instead of a person - and it works
- M. A different kind of cage, but the walls might shape her into something that can break out. She believed it
