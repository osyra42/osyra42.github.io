## About Operation Chimera
> If you have ever seen me do VTubing, my character model I use is based off of the main character of this book. 

> **Operation Chimera** is an ongoing collection of sci-fi noir stories. Where Osyra getting pulled into the Chimera Project - a secret program that turns people into engineered hybrid operatives. It's dark, the choices aren't clean, and the conspiracy unwinds slowly.
 
When she was younger, she was sold to a facility by the family of a foster care program. This is where she was experimenting on Then after that, she was found by the agency trained into a weapon to hunt against cryptids, the unknown, and explore liminal spaces. 

Because of her unique look. People do not like to approach her. This makes a lot of the missions more difficult than necessary when she's trying to save civilians. We'll also look at the introspective thoughts on the subject.

# Rev4
**Outline (First-Person Past Tense):**

I was born to poor parents
- My father was always trying to fix the house. My mother was always trying to clean it.
- they were loving but destitute
- The main reason why we were split was money. We were told it was some sort of abuse. I was never hurt though.

I was put into the foster program
- sold to a medical experiment
- I was injected with Chimera-9
- I survived the infection
- Then the facility was ambushed

I was found by an agency
- trained for extermination
- I was a proficient operator so I stayed solo as Chimera One team, callsign Lone Wolf
- I saw corruption in the agency

I staged a coup
- I got possession of the agency and labs
- I realized I had always been working for the same company as the labs
- I decided to redesign the agency

I started recruiting Chimeran members
- I designed KOMS — "Kaleidoscope Operation Management System". KOMS is an app operators use to carry out missions
- Welcome to Operation Kaleidoscope.
