# 📝 Homeschool Worksheets
::toc::
> ⚠️ **No longer maintained.** These worksheet generators are kept online for anyone still using them, but they are no longer actively developed or updated. Newer tools may do the job better.

Free, printable worksheet generators I built for homeschool families. Each one randomizes its problems, so you get a fresh set every time you click Generate. Print them out, check answers on screen, and move on - no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

It all runs in your browser. Download the zip, extract it, and double-click the launcher.

## 📐 Math

**Currency** - Money math with real-world coin and bill counting, making change, and price calculations.

**Coordinates** - Plotting and identifying points on a coordinate grid.

**Measurements** - Unit conversions and measurement problems across length, weight, and volume.

## How to Use

1. Download the zip file above
2. Extract the folder
3. Run **zlauncher.bat** (Windows) or **zlauncher.sh** (Mac/Linux)
4. Your browser opens the worksheet menu
5. Pick a subject, configure options, and hit Generate
6. Print or save as PDF

> The launcher checks your system and opens the worksheets in your default browser. No installation required.

---

## Notes

The **Definitions** worksheet needs an internet connection to fetch word definitions from a free dictionary API. All other worksheets work fully offline.

The cursive handwriting worksheet uses the Dancing Script font, which is bundled in the download - no internet needed.
