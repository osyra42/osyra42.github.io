## 🤝 Other Ways to Support

Not everything costs money - engagement helps just as much, and most of it is free.

### 📺 Follow & Engage

Follow on [Twitch](https://twitch.tv/osyra42), subscribe on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@osyra42), and hang out on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Xc9TrxtuuE). Likes, comments, and just being in chat all boost visibility - and cost nothing.

Passing a project along to someone who'd find it useful counts too. Word of mouth is real support.

### 💬 Feedback

Honest feedback is some of the most valuable support there is. Bug reports, feature requests, typo catches, or a plain "this part confused me" all help me sharpen the work.

You don't need to be polite about it - blunt is better. Send it however's easiest: a message on [Discord](https://discordapp.com/users/163839418623459330), a reply during a stream, or an email to [CoffeeByteDev@proton.me](mailto:CoffeeByteDev@proton.me).
