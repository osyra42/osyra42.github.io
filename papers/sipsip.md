# 🧋 Sip Sip
::toc::
A little party card game for the table. Pick some decks, draw a card, and either do what it says or take a sip. That's the whole game.

> ℹ️ **Sip what?** Anything you like - it's a quiet gesture of guilt, not a shot list. Coffee, soda, water, or whatever's in your cup all count.

## ▶️ Play

Sip Sip runs right in your browser - nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

**[🧋 Open Sip Sip](sipsip/index.html)**

## 🃏 How It Works

1. **Pick your decks** - turn on as many as you want; the cards get shuffled together
2. **Draw a card** - tap the draw pile to flip the top card
3. **Follow it or sip** - do what the card says, or take a sip if you'd rather not
4. **Keep going** - drawn cards move to the discard pile; play as long as the table's having fun

## 📚 The Decks

Each deck is a different flavor of prompt, so you can tune the night to the room:

- **Friends** - inside jokes, group-chat callouts, and light roasts
- **Family** - table-safe prompts for mixed company
- **Couples** - for partners playing together
- **Food** - eating habits, hot takes, and guilty pleasures
- **Anime** - for the weebs at the table
- **Movies** - film buffs and casual watchers alike
- **Games** - for the gamers
- **Code** - a dev-flavored deck for the programmers

> ⚠️ **Grown-ups only:** the **Buzzed** and **Sex** decks lean adult. Leave them off for family or mixed groups.
